# Attribution

For original educational content under CC BY 4.0, use: “Accounting Agents contributors, ‘[page or record title],’ release [release ID], [canonical URL], CC BY 4.0.” Indicate whether and how you changed the material. Preserve a named creator if a record identifies one.

For a bulk reuse, a consolidated credit is reasonable: “Contains original educational material from Accounting Agents, release [release ID], https://accounting-agents.madebyhenry.chatgpt.site, CC BY 4.0. Changes: [summary].”

CC0 metadata and synthetic fixtures do not require attribution. A courtesy citation to the release and manifest helps others reproduce the work. Software copies or substantial portions must preserve the MIT copyright and permission notice.

Attribution is identification, not endorsement.
