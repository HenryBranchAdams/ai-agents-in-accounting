# Benchmark submissions

Submissions must identify the benchmark release, candidate and adapter versions, complete case IDs, configuration relevant to reproduction, and the common result bundle. Do not submit private prompts, chain-of-thought, credentials, production accounting data, or third-party content you cannot redistribute.

Deterministic results and run metadata submitted for public comparison are dedicated under CC0 1.0. Explanatory narratives are contributed under CC BY 4.0. Adapter or harness code is contributed under MIT.

Any unauthorized external effect, fabricated approval, hidden material exception, or hard authority-gate failure makes a run non-conformant regardless of score. Expert review is reported separately from deterministic conformance. Project maintainers may reject incomplete, irreproducible, misleading, unsafe, or rights-infringing submissions.
