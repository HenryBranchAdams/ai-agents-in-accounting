# DesignCode quality fidelity record

Date: 2026-08-30
Result: accepted for local review

## Reference and translation

The quality reference is Meng To's DesignCode 5 announcement and the official
DesignCode home and Codex Masterclass surfaces. The implementation translates
their precision, first-viewport confidence, deep hierarchy, composed media,
and responsive craft into a light, source-linked accounting knowledge
instrument. It does not copy the course's dark palette, sales mechanics, or
commercial proof.

## Fidelity ledger

1. **Global shell.** The two-rule masthead, compact custom mark, centered
   learning routes, search utility, live-signal indicator, and contextual
   second rail reproduce the reference's disciplined navigation density.
2. **Typography.** The homepage uses a two-line editorial promise at desktop,
   a compact two-line promise at 390 px, restrained reading measure, mono
   metadata, and high-contrast black/indigo hierarchy.
3. **Composed first viewport.** The hero pairs the promise and primary action
   with a code-native corpus compass built from maintained counts. The next
   learning surface enters the desktop viewport without colliding with the
   hero.
4. **Signal and state.** Indigo is reserved for selected paths, focus, current
   navigation, and source-linked actions. Hairlines and open rails replace a
   generic card grid; motion confirms state and is removed for reduced-motion
   users.
5. **Documentation system.** Interior pages use prioritized section
   navigation, a broad reading column, structured provenance band, and a
   contextual right rail. The 390 px shell removes side rails while retaining
   the full 43-destination Explore disclosure.
6. **Atlas workspace.** Industry, time, view, and path controls form one
   instrument rail above coordinated map/list and inspector surfaces. Phone
   layouts use the linear equivalent by default; map zoom and selection remain
   explicitly available.
7. **Responsive legibility.** At 390 px the masthead is compact, actions retain
   44 px targets, path selectors are full width, tables preserve explicit
   overflow containers, and no page depends on a tiny graph for navigation.

## Interaction acceptance

- Search opened as a modal, focused its search field, and closed with Escape.
- Role and industry selection updated the path explanation and the filtered
  Observatory destination.
- Mobile Explore opened a bounded scroll surface containing every
  documentation destination and exactly one current-page marker.
- Atlas Map/List changes updated visible content, pressed state, and URL.
- The default Atlas node is presented as the selected step in a neutral guided
  sequence; no earlier step is shown as completed without explicit progress.
- Homepage, documentation, and Atlas views each retained one `main`, one `h1`,
  semantic navigation, and zero document-width overflow at measured sizes.

## Remaining intentional deviations

- The phone hero omits the large corpus compass so the promise, action, and
  path controls remain the first useful reading sequence.
- The full Atlas map fits maintained graph records rather than the concept's
  smaller illustrative set; zoom controls and the equivalent list are the
  readable detail paths.
- No hosted, deployed, independent-accessibility, or production qualification
  is claimed by this local comparison.
