# Additional editorial header options

![Sixteen additional editorial header options](./editorial-image-options.webp)

Sixteen text-free, exact 3:1 compositions extending the Accounting Agents editorial image system. Production files are 1,800 × 600 progressive JPEGs in `public/images/editorial/options/`. These are a selection library; the original six live placements remain unchanged.

## Shared direction

Every option uses photographed ivory paper or vellum, charcoal rules and registration marks, a restrained forest-green path (`#176b4d`), tactile shadows, Swiss museum-catalog restraint, and generous whitespace. The exclusion list remains: legible text, logos, watermarks, robots, brains, coins, calculators, dashboards, and science-fiction imagery.

## Prompt briefs

All assets were generated with the built-in image-generation workflow. Each brief inherited the shared direction above.

1. **Authority ladder:** rising paper platforms; green route pauses at a brass review point before the final boundary.
2. **Reconciliation tie-out:** opposing ledger strips; supported matches meet at a central seam; one offset timing item.
3. **Period close:** workpaper folders and cutoff slips across a faint calendar grid; review loop; closed archive packet.
4. **Journal-entry assembly:** source slips and calculation fragments converge into a balanced two-column workpaper; final review pin.
5. **Exception triage:** uniform evidence slips with three raised exceptions isolated into a separate review tray.
6. **Cash evidence:** bank and book ledger bands bridged by matched vellum tabs; one suspended timing item.
7. **Revenue layers:** contract cards, performance-obligation packets, and horizontal timing bands; milestones end at review.
8. **Tax filing boundary:** jurisdiction tabs and source schedules meet a mechanical approval clasp before outbound filing.
9. **Audit sampling:** a paper-tile population with a small pinned sample and one separately retained anomaly.
10. **Segregation of duties:** independent source and control lanes cannot cross before joint approval.
11. **Evaluation rig:** repeated workpaper fixtures across a test matrix; consistent results pass while one shifts aside.
12. **Provenance chain:** source, extraction, calculation, and reviewed workpaper connected by a continuous ribbon; versions below.
13. **Secure tool interface:** narrow ports admit only scoped paper chits; a wider untrusted packet remains outside.
14. **Professional learning:** layered workpapers gain structure and reviewability; green pins mark increasing ownership.
15. **Model lifecycle:** current, canary, and retired paper modules distinguish operational state without labels.
16. **Machine-readable interface:** evidence passes through extraction apertures and emerges as structured punched paper.
