# ASC Scope Navigator: starter interview

133 original proposed questions across 13 modules. Prepared September 22, 2026.

**Design status:** These are activity-screening and context prompts with research-routing hints, not a complete ASC inventory or approved accounting rules. A topic hint does not establish applicability. Exact current scope, exceptions, operative versions, and downstream requirements must be authored and independently reviewed.

Ask context first. Core screens may be combined into accessible activity selectors. Present technical and specialist branches only when needed, but always retain unknowns, residual activity checks, and unsupported-coverage notices. Do not ask this entire document as a flat questionnaire.

## Interpretation guardrails
Unknown never means no. No current activity never proves no opening balance, surviving obligation, or comparative disclosure. Industry tags do not exclude general Topics. Policy elections require independent eligibility and adoption analysis. Every mapping is draft and requires current ASC verification.
