# Content Roadmap: AI Agents in Accounting

**Purpose:** Convert the existing standards-grade corpus into the leading educational and working reference for accounting agents.  
**Scope:** Editorial, curriculum, reference, and knowledge-product work only.

---

## Strategic rule

Do not optimize the next release for a larger corpus count.

Optimize for:

1. a reader understanding the field;
2. a practitioner completing a useful task;
3. a reviewer challenging agent-produced work;
4. a builder implementing the accounting boundary correctly;
5. a researcher tracing a claim to appropriate evidence.

---

## Sequence 1 — Establish the learning front door

### C-001 — Publish a five-minute orientation

**Outcome:** A new visitor understands what an accounting agent is, why accounting work is different, and where to start.

**Required content**

- one-sentence definition;
- agent versus chat, copilot, and fixed workflow;
- the governing rule on preparation, approval, and action;
- the evidence-to-decision chain;
- one concrete accounting scenario;
- five audience paths;
- next recommended page for each path.

**Acceptance criteria**

- readable without AI or engineering background;
- no protocol or benchmark detail required;
- includes a two-minute knowledge check;
- links to one guided case, not directly to the 60-record catalog;
- can stand alone as a canonical answer to “What is an accounting agent?”

---

### C-002 — Add role-based learning paths

**Paths**

1. Accounting practitioner
2. Finance transformation leader
3. Agent builder
4. Risk, controls, and assurance
5. Researcher and educator

**Acceptance criteria**

Each path has:

- intended audience;
- prerequisites;
- learning outcomes;
- 5–8 ordered items;
- expected completion artifact;
- optional advanced branch;
- clear exit into real work.

---

### C-003 — Separate content modes

Classify major pages as:

- Tutorial
- How-to
- Explanation
- Reference
- Case study
- Evidence synthesis
- Program documentation

**Acceptance criteria**

- each major page has one primary mode;
- tutorials do not become reference dumps;
- how-to guides are organized around reader goals;
- reference records remain concise and structured;
- explanation pages answer a clear “why” question;
- cross-links connect the modes without mixing them.

---

### C-004 — Reframe the homepage

**Primary thesis**

> The model is not the accounting agent. The accounting agent is the governed system that turns evidence into reviewable work.

**Primary action**

Start the guided bank-reconciliation case.

**Secondary action**

Browse the workflow atlas.

**Acceptance criteria**

- the project thesis appears before corpus counts;
- role-based routes are visible;
- machine access is a utility, not a primary learning path;
- the homepage contains one real accounting example;
- “full” or “complete” coverage claims are qualified by an explicit coverage map.

## Sequence 2 — Build the canonical cases

### C-005 — Canonical case: bank reconciliation

**Required package**

- company and period context;
- complete synthetic bank and ledger evidence;
- missing statement page;
- exact and suggested matches;
- timing items;
- duplicate book entries;
- bank fees;
- unexplained material wire;
- agent specification;
- deterministic checks;
- authority map;
- completed reconciliation;
- proposed journal entries;
- exception log;
- reviewer disposition;
- benchmark cases;
- teaching notes.

**Acceptance criteria**

- a learner can complete the case without external data;
- the case demonstrates a stop condition;
- the final workpaper can be independently reproduced;
- no action is implied without attributable approval;
- every core concept links back to its reference page.

---

### C-006 — Canonical case: accrual and proposed journal entry

**Required concepts**

- completeness of search population;
- service period and cutoff;
- estimate method;
- foreign currency;
- prior accrual and reversal;
- nonresponse from budget owner;
- unsupported estimate;
- proposed entry and reversal;
- reviewer challenge;
- subsequent true-up.

**Acceptance criteria**

- includes a wrong-period source;
- includes one judgment that cannot be reduced to arithmetic;
- ties the proposed entry to support;
- distinguishes preparation, approval, posting, and subsequent validation;
- includes an example of a plausible but unacceptable agent answer.

---

### C-007 — Canonical case: technical accounting memorandum

**Required concepts**

- approved fact pattern;
- missing and disputed facts;
- authoritative hierarchy;
- applicability;
- alternatives;
- contrary authority;
- judgment;
- proposed conclusion;
- reviewer questions;
- policy and disclosure implications.

**Acceptance criteria**

- every material claim has a source and applicability note;
- the case includes an attractive but inapplicable source;
- facts, assumptions, claims, judgments, and decisions are visibly separate;
- the final output is a complete reviewer-ready memo;
- the benchmark allows more than one defensible formulation where appropriate.

---

### C-008 — Canonical case: ICFR control review and test

**Required concepts**

- risk and assertion;
- control objective;
- population;
- control precision;
- evidence;
- walkthrough;
- performance;
- reviewer;
- testing;
- exceptions;
- deficiency evaluation boundary;
- no self-assurance.

**Acceptance criteria**

- the agent may prepare but not make the final effectiveness conclusion;
- source completeness is tested;
- one exception is a documentation issue and another is an operating exception;
- the case demonstrates why performance and assessment must remain separate;
- completed control-design and test templates are included.

---

### C-009 — Canonical adversarial case: vendor bank change and payment

**Required concepts**

- independent verification;
- duplicate vendor indicators;
- sanctions screening;
- malicious instruction in a document;
- requester/approver conflict;
- exact payload approval;
- idempotency;
- bank acknowledgment;
- reconciliation;
- incident path.

**Acceptance criteria**

- contains at least one hard authority failure;
- demonstrates the commit boundary end to end;
- separates master-data change from payment release;
- includes rollback or compensation analysis;
- includes both safe refusal and safe completion episodes.

**Acceptance criteria**

## Sequence 4 — Turn governance into a teachable system

### C-014 — Publish the Accounting Agent Control Model

**Nine elements**

1. Objective
2. Scope
3. Evidence
4. Procedure
5. Checks
6. Authority
7. Review
8. Action
9. Record

**Acceptance criteria**

- one canonical page defines the model;
- adjacent governance pages apply rather than restate it;
- one journal-entry scenario and one payment scenario illustrate all nine elements;
- a printable one-page reference is available;
- every workflow maps to the model.

---

### C-015 — Create the authority decision tree

**Questions**

- Is the action informational, preparatory, recommendatory, executable, representational, or certifying?
- Is it reversible?
- Does it affect records, cash, legal obligations, external parties, master data, or access?
- Is the exact action deterministically constrainable?
- Is approval attributable and payload-bound?
- Is the responsibility inherently human-owned?

**Acceptance criteria**

- includes common misclassifications;
- shows mixed-level workflows;
- explains A3 versus A4;
- never treats confidence as authority;
- maps to sensitive-action examples.

---

### C-016 — Publish the reviewer’s field guide

**Content**

- how to review evidence-linked work;
- how to challenge completeness;
- how to detect fabricated or inapplicable support;
- how to review deterministic checks;
- how to inspect exceptions and contradictions;
- how to assess materiality and judgment;
- how to approve, modify, reject, or escalate;
- common automation-bias traps;
- minimum reviewer packet.

**Acceptance criteria**

- includes good and bad examples;
- includes a review checklist;
- includes a short reviewer calibration exercise;
- works across reconciliation, accrual, research, and control cases.

## Editorial release gate

A content release should not be described as an educational expansion solely because the corpus count increased.

At least one of the following must improve:

- a learner can acquire a new skill;
- a practitioner can complete a concrete task;
- a reviewer can challenge work more effectively;
- a builder can implement a safer boundary;
- a leader can make a better adoption decision;
- a researcher can trace a claim more reliably.

---

2. Revised homepage

4. Accounting Agent Control Model
