# Part XIV — Resourcing

## 60. Core team

A credible first-class program requires, at minimum:

- program director;
- benchmark architect;
- measurement scientist or statistician;
- accounting domain lead;
- evaluation-infrastructure lead;
- environment engineers;
- security and sandbox engineer;
- data and rights lead;
- submission and review operations;
- paid practitioner task authors and reviewers.

## 61. Expert network

Maintain panels across:

- controllership;
- audit and assurance;
- internal controls;
- technical accounting;
- revenue;
- treasury;
- tax;
- inventory and cost accounting;
- financial reporting;
- systems and data;
- multiple industries.

Experts should be compensated, calibrated, and subject to conflict rules.

## 62. Funding principles

Funding should support:

- task creation;
- human baselines;
- infrastructure;
- audits;
- reviewer compensation;
- legal and rights work;
- public reporting.

Sponsors receive no privileged result treatment, hidden-task access, or control over scoring.
