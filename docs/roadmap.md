## Continuing roadmap

The next work is evidence closure within the existing declared universe. Prioritize the following without treating the current assessment as exhaustive knowledge:

1. **Authority and consequential conflicts.** Obtain permitted access to current consolidated requirements for the selected reporting basis; resolve the construction tax-source conflicts and confirm local adoption/effective periods. Preserve exact source versions and the public-evidence limits until then.
2. **Unresolved and shallow source checks.** Revisit the 50 unresolved inherited-source attempts and deepen relevant abstract/landing-only checks. Keep reachability, source currency and substantive review separate. Resolve TabFormer dataset permissions and FiFAR/BAF upstream license conflicts through actual rights evidence.
3. **Transaction-level depth.** Expand the highest-reuse open industry questions with rights-cleared ledger, contract, correction/deletion, cutoff and population-completeness evidence. The original synthetic construction and contrasting-industry examples provide inspectable arithmetic, not proof of real-system performance.
4. **Specialized roles and residual industries.** Use the individual exception reviews to select specific branches in financial institutions, healthcare, government, resources and heterogeneous services. Choose entity form, reporting framework, jurisdiction and period before seeking a sufficient answer.
5. **Jurisdiction expansion and maintenance.** Add demanded state/local and country packages with reviewed source chains and edition-specific classification relations. The two verified relationships do not constitute complete SIC/NACE/ISIC/NAICS crosswalks. Verify hosted CI and real scheduled source-refresh execution when publication work is separately authorized.

The reading surface, exact research filters, API/CLI/MCP retrieval, review ledgers, coverage downloads and source archive support this continuing work. No additional course, execution, lab or benchmark product is planned. Specialist sign-off, private data, purchased access and publication have not occurred.

**1. Establish the review standard and reconcile existing material**

Make the existing corpus a dependable basis for expansion. Review candidate mappings, triage the 215 question-unassigned records and inspect industry-unassigned records for specific or shared relevance. Some records should remain unassigned with an explanation. Reverify the 488 inherited source references in batches, beginning with authorities and sources reused by many workflows; review inherited editorial records against their supporting sources.

For each source, establish the original publisher, relevant edition and locator, jurisdiction/framework, effective period, supersession, actual access/review scope and rights evidence. Inaccessible or uncertain material retains its limitation. Resolve the dataset permission questions already named in the catalog, including TabFormer and FiFAR/BAF, without inferring rights from availability.

Define a reusable research unit: an answerable question for stated business facts, reporting basis, jurisdiction and period. Record applicability separately from evidence status. Support applicable, conditional, unassessed and reasoned not-applicable dispositions; attach conditions and evidence. Maintain a frozen question list for each assessment so selecting only easy questions cannot improve a completeness measure.

The existing coverage files can support much of this work. Add narrowly scoped metadata for applicability, question inventories and detailed-industry exception review where the current contract is insufficient. Preserve independent professional-review, empirical-evidence, currency and rights dimensions.

Completion evidence: every baseline record has been triaged with a recorded disposition; candidate associations have a review outcome; source dependencies for the next research release have adequate review evidence or explicit unresolved limitations. The full inherited review queue can continue alongside expansion, but a new sourced answer must not silently depend on an unchecked claim.

**2. Build shared foundations and establish construction depth**

Review all 62 question families and define the common research questions within them. Prioritize foundations reused broadly: reporting basis, close, revenue, receivables/payables, cash, payroll, inventory and costing, assets/leases, tax, consolidation, controls, data lineage and agent evidence. Extend rather than duplicate the workflows, guides and sources already present.

Nine families currently have no mapped material: subsequent events/going concern; employee benefits; equity and complex capital; share-based compensation; derivatives/hedging; cross-border tax; restructuring/insolvency; biological/extractive resources; and licensed content/royalties. Inspect existing records before declaring a source gap. The other 53 families also need depth assessment.

Use construction to establish the first repeatable example of depth. Connect contract facts and modifications to estimate versions, job-cost transactions, progress measures, WIP, billings, retainage, AP/payroll, the general ledger and cash reconciliation. Add rights-cleared or clearly labeled original examples with numerical tie-outs. Cover estimate revisions, disputed modifications, uninstalled materials, loss contracts, retainage and reopened jobs as distinct questions. Synthetic examples illustrate reasoning; they cannot substitute for real transaction or deployment evidence.

Review current consolidated authority for the declared scope. Record unresolved source conflicts, including the tax-transition questions already identified. Separate building construction, heavy/civil work and specialty trades; distinguish contractors from developers and property owners. Add state and local branches through a declared operating profile, initially following the existing construction work before broadening the footprint.

Completion evidence: the chosen construction question set has explicit applicability, traced answers, worked material where relevant, reconciliations, review controls and known exceptions. Seek named professional review for consequential accounting/legal interpretations and record the actual result. Missing professional review or real-system evidence remains visible as its own gap. Do not promote the existing partial assessment simply because additional records were added.

**3. Expand through contrasting business models**

Start the next industry wave with manufacturing, wholesale/retail, software/subscriptions and professional services. This order is an editorial recommendation based on reusable accounting patterns and contrast with construction, not an economic ranking. Pick explicit subsectors and business profiles before each pass.

Each pass produces an applicability profile, an inventory of answerable questions, reviewed links to shared foundations, original-source evidence, an industry brief and workflow detail, worked material where useful, exception notes and a gap assessment. Review data inputs and controls alongside accounting treatment.

Use these first contrasting profiles to measure research effort and revise the standard. A manufacturing review should challenge whether the construction approach handles conversion costs and inventory; a software review should challenge whether it distinguishes the provider's revenue from the customer's software expenditure. Unfamiliar multi-activity businesses should expose missing branches in the map.

Completion evidence: selected profiles satisfy the same explicit research criteria, their detailed-industry branches have been inspected, and representative searches retrieve the right material without transferring authority across incompatible frameworks or roles. Outcomes from this wave determine the schedule for broader expansion.

**5. Extend frameworks and jurisdictions systematically**

Build explicit jurisdiction/framework profiles where they change an answer. Start with US federal and selected state/local branches tied to declared use cases; progressively inventory the remaining states and local obligations relevant to those profiles. Do not imply that a US industry review is a 50-state legal, tax or payroll survey.

Extend internationally through verified, versioned classification correspondences and accounting-framework mappings. The existing research proposes SIC for SEC discovery, ISIC as an international bridge and NACE for EU activity coverage; complete crosswalks are not implemented. Preserve each scheme's edition and relation type, and verify current official publications at implementation time.

Choose country/framework packages by actual research demand, material gaps and access to authoritative evidence. IFRS coverage does not automatically establish local tax, payroll, company law or locally adopted reporting requirements. Add local differences and effective periods explicitly. Keep NAICS edition migration as a separate maintenance task when an adopted successor is available.

Completion evidence: each published jurisdiction package declares its activity/entity/framework/period scope, sources, exceptions and unresolved questions. Classification correspondence provides discovery links; it does not establish equivalent accounting treatment.

**Continuing work across every phase**

- **Real evidence and permissions.** Seek reusable transaction, ledger, contract and reconciliation examples; document corrections, deletions, cutoff, missing populations and extraction lineage. Collect independent deployment studies, incidents and contrary findings. Review published empirical claims and reproducibility without turning the corpus into an execution or benchmarking service. Real data, full-text access and professional review may require outside participation; keep those dependencies explicit.
- **Retrieval usefulness.** Add realistic positive and scope-counterexample questions with each accepted research package. Extend agent discovery/context to include reviewed industry/question scope and material omissions where needed. Verify a reader or connected agent can find an answer, trace it to the publisher, distinguish inapplicable guidance and recognize a gap. Existing test fixtures verify retrieval mechanics, not answer correctness or real-world agent performance. Add ranking or semantic-search complexity only in response to observed failures.
- **Maintenance and release reliability.** Verify the configured CI and scheduled source-health workflow against the actual maintained repository. Ensure bounded checks rotate through the population and trigger an editorial review queue; reachability never upgrades source review. Prioritize changing authority, known conflicts and heavily reused evidence. Preserve immutable snapshots, source provenance, rights, generated-download integrity and accurate current documentation with each authorized publication.

**What qualifies a research question as sufficiently covered**

Use the existing seven depth dimensions, with a rationale for dimensions that do not apply. A qualifying assessment should identify its scope and accounting question; cite authority and effective requirements appropriate to that scope; describe needed evidence inputs, workflow and controls where relevant; include worked material where it materially supports the answer; and disclose empirical support or its absence. The evidence must support the actual conclusion, including conditions and exceptions. Record review depth and rights separately. A source count, reviewed association, passing test or reachable page cannot satisfy these criteria by itself.

Professional review and empirical performance are additional evidence dimensions, not consequences of an editorial sufficiency label. Publish precise claims such as “sourced research answer for this scope” and “reviewed by this named specialist” only when the associated evidence exists.

**Workload and sequencing**

The known program is 62 family reviews, 96 subsector profiles and 1,012 detailed-industry exception checks, with 715 inherited records to triage/reverify and continuing source intake. Shared questions and sources reduce duplication. The 5,952 screening pairs are not 5,952 separate literature reviews. The number of answerable questions, authority checks and jurisdiction variants will become clearer during the pilots.

This is a program of research releases. Software work is bounded and should follow demonstrated needs. Research depth, access to current authorities, reusable data and specialist review determine much of the total effort. A calendar or budget for exhaustive coverage would be speculative before measuring the first representative packages. Record researcher effort, new-versus-reused evidence, review turnaround, unresolved dependencies and rework during the pilots, then forecast the remaining work by profile complexity.

The recommended next release combines the review standard and priority mapping cleanup with the construction contract-to-WIP-to-ledger package. Begin high-reuse foundation/source review alongside it. Follow with the contrasting-model wave, then extend the same measured process across the full inventory. International and state expansion can advance where demand is clear while the wider US program continues.
