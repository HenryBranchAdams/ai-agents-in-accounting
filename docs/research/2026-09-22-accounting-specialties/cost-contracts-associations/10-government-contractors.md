# 10. Government contractors

Research date: 2026-09-22 America/Chicago; selected retrievals occurred 2026-09-23 UTC. Scope: US federal commercial procurement, particularly cost-type and DoD clause settings. Applicability depends on executed contract clauses, agency supplements, contractor business unit, contract type and CAS coverage. Procurement reimbursement/pricing and accounting-system rules do not automatically establish GAAP recognition.

## Baseline and current coverage

Baseline-matched guides include `guide-q-project-wip` and `guide-q-cost-allocation`; questions include `rq-us-project-wip-contract-costs`, `rq-cost-allocation-allocation-base`, `rq-us-professional-services-reimbursables`, `rq-us-professional-services-fixed-fee-wip`, and `rq-us-project-wip-over-time-cutoff`. Existing sources include FAR Part 31, DCAA preaward accounting-system material, WIP/revenue sources and construction payment clauses. They do not give an integrated contractor timekeeping, indirect-rate, incurred-cost and contract-billing workflow. DCAA’s ICE page is accessible only as a restricted/inaccessible locator in this research; separate public DCAA pamphlet excerpts and the official DFARS clause add substantive, scoped evidence [cca_dcaaice, cca_dcaa_timekeeping, cca_dfars_2522427006].

## Findings

**Allowability and allocation.** FAR 31.201-2 requires a claimed cost to be reasonable, allocable, compliant with applicable CAS or GAAP/practice where appropriate, permitted by the contract, and not barred by a cost principle. Records must support incurrence, allocability and compliance. FAR 31.202 bars inconsistent treatment of like costs as direct and indirect; FAR 31.203 requires logical pools and allocation bases reflecting benefit to cost objectives. FAR 31.201-1 permits use of standard costs for contract total-cost computation only with applicable variance adjustment. The specific clause set, cost principle, CAS coverage and award date matter [cca_far31].

**System, cost ledger and billing control.** DFARS 252.242-7006 lists criteria for an acceptable accounting system when incorporated into a DoD contract. It calls for direct/indirect segregation, direct accumulation by contract, logical indirect allocation, general-ledger control and reconciliation, documented adjustments and management review, labor identified by cost objective and distributed correctly, monthly contract-cost determination, exclusion of FAR Part 31 unallowables, rate-calculation support, and billings reconcilable to cost accounts. Its final criterion applies CAS when applicable and otherwise GAAP. This clause is strong source-backed operational detail but must not be generalized to every civilian agency or contractor without confirming applicability [cca_dfars_2522427006].

**Timekeeping and labor.** DFARS 252.242-7006, when incorporated into a DoD contract, requires a timekeeping system that identifies employee labor by intermediate or final cost objective and a labor distribution system that charges direct and indirect labor to appropriate objectives. FAR Part 31 separately requires adequate cost support and consistent direct/indirect treatment. The DCAA contractor pamphlet could not be opened; snippets surfaced by search are discovery only and do not substantiate any procedural claim here. Detailed labor transfers, paid leave, indirect labor, subcontractor hours, corrections, uncompensated overtime and specific CAS requirements need accessible source review [cca_dfars_2522427006, cca_far31].

**Indirect rates and incurred-cost proposals.** Billing rates are provisional interim rates subject to adjustment. FAR Subpart 42.7 and FAR 52.216-7 set final indirect-rate and incurred-cost proposal duties for covered contracts; generally the certified final proposal is due within six months after fiscal-year end unless a written extension is approved in exceptional circumstances. The schedule reconciles pools and allocation bases to the books and contract allocations; final settled rates true up amounts billed and support closeout. DCAA ICE’s listing identifies an Excel schedule tool, but its workbook was inaccessible, so no template or adequacy conclusion is asserted [cca_far42, cca_far2167, cca_dcaaice].

**Billing mechanism matters.** FAR 52.216-7 reimburses allowable incurred costs under cost-reimbursement terms, with provisional indirect billings and later settlement. FAR 52.232-16 is contract financing for defined fixed-price settings: the clause computes progress payments using specified incurred costs, a percentage and exclusions/limits, and does not turn financing cash into GAAP revenue. Construction progress payments, performance-based payments and deliverable invoices have distinct procedures. Use the executed clause/alternate and award vintage [cca_far2167, cca_far23216].

**GAAP bridge.** Job-cost ledger, indirect pools, allowable-cost schedules, financing, invoices, receivables, contract assets/liabilities and revenue are different views. Topic 606/340-40 and other relevant US GAAP govern financial reporting where applicable; FAR allowability answers whether a cost can be reimbursed/priced under the contract, not when to recognize GAAP revenue, capitalize a cost or show a contract balance. Map source evidence through payroll/material/subcontract capture, cost objective, ledger reconciliation, rate schedule, invoice and rate true-up, then separately reconcile GAAP balances [cca_far31, cca_far2167, cca_far23216, cca_dfars_2522427006].

## Recommended additions

- Add a contractor close workflow keyed by entity/business unit, clause/CAS coverage, cost objective, labor controls, pools/bases, provisional rates, incurred-cost proposal, audit adjustments, invoices and closeout.
- Review current CAS Board text and DCAA audit-program materials for timekeeping and incurred-cost adequacy; do not present indexed pamphlet excerpts as a full-read operational manual.
- Add a diagram or worked reconciliation separating cost-reimbursement, cost-based financing, fixed-price deliverable billing and GAAP contract balances.

## Unresolved gaps

ICE workbook/user instructions remain inaccessible. The DCAA timekeeping pamphlet is only partially inspected from indexed official excerpts; exact page context, current edition and specific edge cases require a full accessible copy. Full/current CAS texts, agency supplements, CAS coverage and cost treatment for selected items need additional primary review. Civilian procurement, grants (2 CFR Part 200), state/local awards and specific executed contract clauses lie outside this package. No contractor ledger, contract or cost submission was inspected.
