# Accounting specialty expansion roadmap

This is a proposed authoring and source-intake sequence, not an implementation or publication record. Research was requested for all 15 areas, so later waves are sequencing choices rather than exclusions. Priority reflects shared foundations, distinct accounting questions, and gaps in the inspected local corpus. It is not a demand forecast or a claim of professional sufficiency.

## 1. Build reusable specialty foundations

| Order | Area | Proposed guide expansion | Why first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Partnership and LLC accounting (1) | Separate financial-statement equity, section 704(b) capital, tax capital and outside basis; then contributions, allocations, distributions and ownership changes. | This prevents recurring confusion in investment partnerships, real estate and family-office work. |
| 2 | Cost and managerial accounting (7) | Cost objects and drivers; job/process/standard/ABC systems; overhead and variance bridges; contribution and allocated profitability. | Extends the existing narrow allocation example and supports manufacturing, contractors, dealerships and service businesses. |
| 3 | Small-business reporting and accountant services (6) | Define the reporting basis and service engagement before preparing a close, adjustments or statements. | Gives the library an accessible baseline for ordinary accounting work and clarifies what assurance a deliverable provides. |
| 4 | Fund accounting and investment partnerships (2) | Fund financial statements, NAV, investor capital rollforward, fee and allocation accounting, calls/distributions and dealing conventions. | Existing custody and family-office references do not replace books at the fund level. Shared foundations should precede separate PE/VC/hedge treatments. |

## 2. Extend the foundations into operating and investment verticals

| Area | Proposed guide/source expansion | Dependencies and reuse |
|---|---|---|
| Private equity, venture capital, hedge funds (8) | Separate fund, manager, GP and portfolio company; distinguish closed-end waterfall mechanics from hedge subscriptions, redemptions, high-water marks and equalization. | Build on areas 1/2. Use distinct accounting examples for PE/VC and hedge arrangements; preserve agreement dependence. |
| Real estate ownership/property management (9) | Owner/developer cost stages, rental accounting and rent roll, tenant deposits, CAM recoveries, manager-to-owner settlement, investment structures and REIT tax reporting. | Reuse `guide-real-estate-us-roles` and lease/capital-asset sources. Add substantive treatment to existing roles rather than a duplicate overview. Partnership foundation supports ownership structures. |
| Government contractors (10) | Job-cost and timekeeping records, direct/indirect costs, provisional versus final rates, billings and incurred-cost reporting. | Build on area 7 and existing construction WIP. Keep contract allowability and reimbursement separate from GAAP recognition. |
| SaaS/subscriptions (13) | Contract/billing/revenue bridge, modifications, usage and credits, commission amortization, provider development versus customer implementation. | Reuse information-provider, revenue and software records. Preserve effective-date and adoption distinctions for software amendments. |
| E-commerce/marketplace sellers (14) | Gross sales-to-payout reconciliation, returns/disputes/chargebacks, inventory held by fulfillment providers, fees and facilitator-tax boundaries. | Reuse trade/revenue/inventory guides and source IDs. Platform documentation supports transaction behavior; separate sources support accounting conclusions. |
| Oil/gas operators and nonoperators (15) | Joint-account charges, working-interest owner ledgers, effective-dated ownership/distributions, financial versus tax depletion and retirement obligations. | Extend `guide-us-extractive-production-settlement`. Keep federal/Indian royalty reporting separate from private mineral rights and GAAP. |

## 3. Add distinct specialty libraries alongside the second wave

These can proceed independently once their source packets are ready; there is no accounting dependency requiring them to wait for every second-wave vertical.

| Area | Proposed guide/source expansion | Critical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Forensic/litigation (3) | Engagement purpose, transaction tracing, loss/damages assumptions, evidence documentation and expert reporting. | A forensic engagement is not an audit opinion, and an evidentiary rule does not prescribe a universal damages method. Separate sourced requirements from editorial workpaper suggestions. |
| Bankruptcy/restructuring (4) | Debtor operating reports and claim reconciliation, reorganization/fresh-start accounting, liquidation-basis recognition and statements. | Court/regulatory schedules, legal claim allowance and GAAP measurements are distinct. Existing exit-cost guidance does not cover them. |
| Employee benefit plans (5) | Plan statements, participant balances, contributions, distributions, investments and audit/reporting routes. | Plan accounting is separate from employer expense; regulatory participant counts and filing categories are not a universal GAAP rule. |
| Auto/equipment dealerships (11) | Inventory/trade-ins, floorplan financing, manufacturer incentives, warranty receivables and service/parts departments. | Dealer-side books must not be inferred from a manufacturer's accounting. Issuer examples are illustrative rather than generally authoritative. |
| HOAs/condominiums (12) | Assessment revenue, operating/reserve funds, special assessments, future-repair information, developer transition and tax election. | State reserve requirements, board budgets, Topic 972/606 and federal tax classification answer different questions. |

## Source-intake sequence

1. **Enrich existing sources first where the same publication is already present.** The inventory records exact and researcher-identified corpus matches. Preserve stable IDs; a new URL or a deeper read does not automatically justify a new source record.
2. **Add substantively inspected sources by concrete question.** Prefer original regulatory/professional/technical materials. Use clearly classified accounting-firm interpretations, issuer examples, original educational works and platform documentation where those are the appropriate evidence type.
3. **Keep restricted and discovery-only leads separate.** They are useful acquisition pointers, not support for accounting claims. No full-text acquisition, license acceptance or blanket reuse permission is part of this handoff.
4. **Author the foundation before the industry specialization when it is shared.** Avoid duplicating partnership capital, fair-value or cost-allocation explanations across multiple verticals. Preserve separate fund, manager, investor, property owner and service-provider examples.
5. **Carry unresolved accounting questions into the future guide.** Licensed current Codification, entity contracts, state applicability and professional judgments remain explicit dependencies, not reasons to describe an entire specialty as complete.
