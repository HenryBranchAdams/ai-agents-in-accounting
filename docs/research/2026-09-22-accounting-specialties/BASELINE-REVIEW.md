Research snapshot: local corpus 2026-09-21.3. The exact Git revision and input hashes are in baseline.json. Initial working tree was clean. No live deployment or remote issue state is inferred.

The baseline contains 767 source records, 373 named questions, and 180 assessments. The 15 screening sets overlap and must not be added together. Classification coverage is not substantive specialty coverage.

## Material distinctions established before dispatch

- Partnership/LLC accounting: `guide-q-equity` addresses instrument classification; it does not supply a partnership capital-account curriculum. `guide-family-office-us-accounting` has K-1/basis coordination and investment notices, useful adjacent context.
- Fund accounting and PE/VC/hedge funds: `guide-q-funds-custody` addresses adviser custody and fee boundaries. It does not establish NAV, allocation, waterfall or equalization treatment. Family-office investor accounting must remain distinct from a fund's own books and manager revenue.
- Forensic/litigation: `guide-q-controls-fraud` provides adjacent control and fraud context. Dedicated investigative and damages research requires separate source work.
- Bankruptcy/restructuring: `guide-q-restructuring-distress` has IFRS restructuring/disposal and selected US exit-cost/discontinued-operation questions. These are not a bankruptcy, fresh-start or liquidation-basis package.
- Benefit plans: `guide-q-benefits` primarily addresses employer obligations, expense and benefit-close boundaries. Plan-level reporting is a distinct research subject.
- Small-business reporting: general reporting-basis and professional-governance families are adjacent. Preparation, compilation, review and special-purpose basis research must be established independently.
- Cost/managerial accounting: `guide-q-cost-allocation` includes a synthetic shared-service pool and allocation-driver sensitivity; `guide-q-planning` includes a budget/forecast bridge. Broader standard, job, process and activity-based costing must not be assumed from these bounded cases.
- Real estate: `guide-real-estate-us-roles` already contains acquisition/development, rent/deposit, sale, property-manager settlement and lessor questions. This area needs depth rather than a claim of no coverage.
- Government contractors: preaward-system and federal cost references exist, but applicability branches in a private manufacturer's example do not establish contractor accounting coverage.
- Dealerships: classification-level motor-vehicle dealer context exists. A profile does not establish floorplan, trade-in, incentives or warranty accounting.
- HOA/condominium: the focused source/guide/question screen found no dedicated coverage. The worker must still verify before finalizing this assessment.
- SaaS: information-provider and software-cost questions exist; research must deepen the contract lifecycle and separate supplier revenue from customer software expenditure.
- E-commerce: trade, inventory, marketplace presentation and settlement questions exist; research must deepen returns, chargebacks, platform settlement and tax boundaries.
- Oil/gas: `guide-us-extractive-production-settlement` already separates production, ownership, settlement and recognition and has cost-stage, retirement, revenue and royalty questions. Joint-interest and owner accounting should extend this work.
