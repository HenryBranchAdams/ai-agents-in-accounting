# 2. Fund accounting and investment partnerships

Research date: September 22, 2026 (America/Chicago). US first. Findings are bounded research, not professional conclusions.

## Baseline and existing records

Baseline matches are broad: `guide-q-investments`, `guide-q-valuation-impairment`, `guide-q-funds-custody`, `guide-aa-i124-institutional`, `guide-aa-i106-finance-insurance`, `guide-family-office-us-accounting`, and several unrelated institutional/industry guides. Assessments explicitly bound some findings to fair value, custody, or K-1 research. These provide useful shared foundations but do not describe a fund/GP/manager chart of accounts, NAV close, investor capital ledger, or fee/waterfall accounting. Key source records include `src_fasb_asc820_fair_value`, `src_aa_i106_sec_2a5_2020`, `src_aa_i124_custody`, `src_family_office_irs_k1_1065_2025`, and the SEC custody source `src_roadmap_sec_custody_2003`.

## Research findings

**Declare the accounting entity and basis before designing the close.** A limited partnership fund, its GP, investment manager/adviser, blockers, parallel funds, co-investment vehicles, and portfolio companies can have different books and reporting duties. A statement from a custodian documents positions/cash held in an account; it does not by itself determine which legal entity owns the asset or whether the account is client custody. Existing custody sources make the regulatory “custody” question distinct from title, beneficial ownership, recognition, or the GAAP consolidation conclusion. For SEC advisers relying on the pooled-vehicle audit provision under Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-2, the rule’s audit and delivery conditions do not erase other custody requirements; assess current rule text, adviser status, vehicle type, and exception facts. [SEC custody rule release, `cp_sec_custody`](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/ia-2176.htm), pooled investment vehicles and “custody” definition; existing `src_aa_i124_custody` and `src_roadmap_sec_custody_2003`.

**NAV is an accounting close output, not an investor capital allocation formula.** For a fund that meets ASC 946’s investment-company scope, the KPMG August 2026 handbook states that portfolio investments are generally measured at fair value because those values are central to investors and net assets. Topic 946 capital transactions are also NAV-oriented: open-end fund share orders are recognized on trade date, while a nonregistered investment partnership records subscriptions/redemptions on the effective date in its governing documents and treats earlier cash as an advance-contribution liability. Compute class-specific NAV and reconcile pending investment transactions, cash, borrowings, accruals and marks. The latter close-control list is an editorial workpaper recommendation; it is not quoted as a standalone standard requirement. SEC Rule 2a-5 applies to registered investment companies and BDCs, not all private partnerships. [KPMG ASC 946 handbook, `cp_kpmg_investment_companies_2026`](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/frv/pdf/2026/handbook-investment-companies.pdf), §§1.1, 4.1-4.2; [SEC Rule 2a-5 guide, `cp_sec_2a5`](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/good-faith-determinations-fair-value-small-entity-compliance-guide), scope and valuation designee. IPEV remains a rights-restricted discovery lead only; no substantive IPEV claim supports this brief.

**Fund-level fee expense and manager-level fee revenue are separate books.** KPMG’s ASC 946 handbook describes management-fee bases such as net assets, commitments or invested capital at contract rates, and covers waivers, expense limitations and offsets. ASC 946 reporting can present fees waived/reimbursed as a reduction of fund expenses and related terms are disclosed; qualifying expense-limitation carryovers may create a liability. In a separate legal manager entity, contractual advisory revenue/receivable and its payroll/vendor costs remain that entity’s accounts; this is an entity-separation recommendation, and intercompany/consolidation analysis still depends on actual control and agreement facts. The fund agreement determines fee bases, step-downs, rebates and side-letter economics. [KPMG ASC 946 handbook, `cp_kpmg_investment_companies_2026`](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/frv/pdf/2026/handbook-investment-companies.pdf), §5.1, fee waivers and offsets. Advisory rules and contract/disclosure obligations are separate from GAAP recognition. The 2023 SEC private-fund quarterly statement, audit, restricted-activity, and preferential-treatment rules were vacated effective June 5, 2024; SEC technical amendments reflecting vacatur became effective November 19, 2024. Do not cite the former requirements as current. Existing custody-rule pooled-fund audited-statement pathways remain a distinct source. [SEC vacatur status, `cp_sec_vacatur`](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2024/11/s7-03-22); [SEC custody rule, `cp_sec_custody`](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/ia-2176.htm).

**Investor capital activity requires commitment, contributed capital, distributions, and NAV attribution to remain distinct.** A commitment is a contractual unfunded amount; a call notice creates a demand under the agreement, while bank settlement proves cash movement. Reconcile total and partner-level commitments, cumulative calls, unfunded commitments, equalization amounts, recallable distributions, transfers, default/late-payment treatment, and returned capital. ILPA’s September 2025 v2.0 capital call/distribution template is an industry reporting format that identifies commitments, unfunded balance, cumulative/current calls/distributions, notice transaction type, and effect on unfunded balance. It is not an accounting standard and its template formulas do not establish recognition. [ILPA template page, `cp_ilpa`](https://ilpa.org/resources-tools/resource-library/ilpa-capital-call-distribution-template-v-2-0/), version 2.0 overview and linked guidance.

**ASC 946 investor capital, incentive allocation, and carry/clawback need separate but reconciled schedules.** KPMG reproduces AICPA TQA 6910.29 for nonregistered investment partnerships reporting investor classes: ending class equity reflects cumulative unrealized gains/losses, carry and clawback as if the fund realized assets and settled liabilities at reported fair values, then allocated/distributed net assets under the partnership documents. Depending on the agreement and arrangement, a manager/GP incentive amount may be presented as fund expense or as a disproportionate allocation of net income from LP to GP capital; ASC 946 disclosures apply. For a concrete contract example, ILPA’s Delaware-based model LPA returns qualifying partner contributions first, then a bracketed 8% annual preferred return compounded annually and calculated daily from each contribution date, then a bracketed GP catch-up until the GP has 20% of cumulative profits, followed by a bracketed 20% GP/80% LP residual split. It also contains interim/final clawback, escrow and LP giveback. A simplified arithmetic illustration of those placeholder terms (no fees, taxes or losses; one $100 contribution outstanding for one year) is $100 returned, $8 preferred return to the LP, then $2 GP catch-up because $2 equals 20% of $8+$2; later profit is split 20/80. This is an editorial illustration calculated from the model formula, not an accounting requirement or signed agreement. Book capital allocations, tax allocations and cash distribution tiers must not be presumed identical. [KPMG ASC 946 handbook, `cp_kpmg_investment_companies_2026`](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/frv/pdf/2026/handbook-investment-companies.pdf), TQA 6910.29; [ILPA deal-by-deal model LPA, `cp_ilpa_model_lpa_deal_by_deal`](https://ilpa.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/ILPA-Model-Limited-Parnership-Agreement-Deal-By-Deal.pdf), arts. 14.3, 14.7-14.12. ILPA’s separate call/distribution template, `cp_ilpa`, provides notice fields only; IRS 2025 Form 1065 instructions, `cp_irs_1065`, supply tax allocation/reporting boundaries.

**Hedge fund statement examples bound several dealing and incentive mechanisms.** KPMG’s 2025 US GAAP illustrative hedge statements show an ASC 946 domestic partnership with monthly partner profit/loss allocations, advance subscriptions as liabilities, fees payable, withdrawals payable, and side-pocket gains/losses allocated only to investors participating when the pocket was formed; pocket interests remain nonredeemable until sale or transferability. Its offshore unitized example reports investor share activity, NAV and performance fees by class/series; the sample fee applies to NAV exceeding the higher of issue price or prior-year high NAV. The partnership illustration separately shows incentive allocation subject to loss carryforward. These are fictional disclosure examples, not current standard-setter interpretations or a universal fund method. KPMG’s 2026 ASC 946 handbook separately explains that performance-fee loss carryforwards reduce future performance fees but do not create a fund asset, and that Topic 946 equalization for investment-company shares can create class/share issuance credits and repurchase debits with separate presentation of the equalization amount. Do not import mutual-fund equalization mechanics into a PE/hedge LPA without document support. [KPMG hedge fund illustrative statements, `cp_kpmg_hedge_illustrative_2025`](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2025/illustrative-financial-statement-hedge-funds-2025.pdf), partner/class-series notes; [KPMG ASC 946 handbook, `cp_kpmg_investment_companies_2026`](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/frv/pdf/2026/handbook-investment-companies.pdf), §5.1, TQA 6910.29, equalization. A conceptual workpaper test is to carry each investor’s share/series high-water threshold, subscriptions/redemptions and fee crystallization separately, then recompute fee eligibility at each defined date; this is an editorial control recommendation, not a standard formula. CFTC commodity-pool, SEC adviser, registered-fund, tax partnership, and financial-reporting status remain separate classification questions.

## Additions and guide shape

Add a US fund close guide with four linked ledgers: (1) fund investments/NAV; (2) partner/member commitments, contributions, distributions and capital accounts; (3) GP/manager fee and expense ledgers; (4) portfolio-company books. Include role and consolidation decision gates, fair-value evidence, custodian reconciliation, side-letter/equalization controls, investor reporting bridge, and tax K-1/inside-outside basis reconciliation. Create separate private-equity/venture and hedge-fund branches rather than one generic NAV recipe.

## Unresolved gaps

- KPMG provides public technical interpretation with cited ASC excerpts and examples, not a substitute for the live Codification or entity-specific review. Current consolidated ASC 820 text and PE/VC security-specific valuation calibration were not established in this pass.
- KPMG/ILPA examples narrow, but do not resolve, contract-specific subscription lines, fee offsets, fund-of-funds consolidation, waterfalls, clawbacks or subscription equalization. No actual LPA, side letter or fund ledger was reviewed.
- IPEV remains a rights-restricted discovery lead only and is not used as substantive support; its terms notice requires rights review before any further access.
- State partnership law, investor side letters, fund accounting administrator policies, and actual valuation files were not available.
- Regulatory branches for CFTC CPO/CTA, registered investment companies, ERISA plan assets, Form PF, and private-offering exemptions need entity-specific current review. Form PF amendments adopted in 2024 have a current compliance date of July 1, 2027 after SEC/CFTC extensions; the Form remains a regulator reporting obligation only for covered filers. See area 8 source `cp_sec_formpf`.
