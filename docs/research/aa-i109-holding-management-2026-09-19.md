# Selected US holding-company and management-entity research

The package connects parent and subsidiary books to a consolidation worksheet, with separate passive-owner, management-service, investee and tax roles. It inventories the one existing NAICS 55 association and 73 linked questions from the selected reusable families. That larger question count includes shared context and is not a measure of industry coverage.

Six answers cover perimeter, investments, financing, services and cost allocation, dividends and tax boundaries, and the closing reconciliation. Existing consolidation, investments, debt, related-party, allocation and tax records remain intact. Three new sources, four original records and six partial assessments are proposed for additive integration.

The selected original evidence is:

- [ARB 51 as amended](https://storage.fasb.org/ARB%2051.pdf), After FAS 160 paragraphs 1-6, 9, 11 and 24 on PDF pages 6-8. This is historical pre-Codification material. The earlier version in the same PDF is a separate passage.
- [2025 Regulation S-X 3A-02](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title17-vol3/pdf/CFR-2025-title17-vol3-sec210-3A-02.pdf), introductory text and paragraph (a), plus adjacent 3A-03(b), printed pages 286-287. The April 1, 2025 edition supports selected registrant perimeter research; it does not supply the intercompany-elimination procedure or establish current GAAP.
- [Controlled-services regulation](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/cfr/26/1?link-type=pdf&sectionnum=482-9&year=mostrecent), 1.482-9(j), (k), (l)(1)-(3)(v), and (b)(7) Example 16. The resolver served the April 1, 2025 edition during review. Its URL can change editions; the exact reviewed period and later-currency uncertainty are recorded. The tax benefit and allocation rules are not a GAAP cost-allocation mandate.
- The existing [2025 Form 1120 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1120) source is reused without changing its primary record. An additional package-scoped review covers Item A, Consolidated Return; Schedule C, Consolidated returns; and Schedule K, Question 3. Tax group qualification and reporting are separate from financial consolidation.

Current eCFR section lookups were inaccessible. Historical or dated public text is retained with that limitation, and all six assessments leave source currency unknown. Accepted ASU 2015-02, ASU 2018-17 and revenue foundations are linked by stable source ID with their existing limits. No publisher full text or new external reuse permission is included.

The original example uses integer cents and shows every journal, counterparty match, elimination and trial balance. It assumes a wholly owned subsidiary formed for cash, confirmed control, a common annual period, no acquisition differences, and a simple loan. Parent standalone input is explicitly a cost-method bookkeeping worksheet, not a complete GAAP standalone statement or a conclusion about the required investment method.

The 200,000-cent shared-service pool allocates 120,000 to the subsidiary and leaves 80,000 with the parent, using assumed documented processing units. The group retains all 200,000 of external pool expense. The 500,000-cent loan accrues 30,000 cents for a full year at 6%. The 100,000-cent dividend is internal. A separate outside-client branch increases group cash to 3,150,000 and profit to 650,000 cents; its revenue survives the rollup.

Counterexamples address passive ownership without a service, an unmatched 20,000-cent loan difference, an investee whose control facts are unknown, a 60% financial-group holding versus the stated 80% tax ownership threshold, and outside-client revenue. Actual control, standalone method, transfer pricing, loan and distribution legality, tax elections, NCI and acquisition adjustments remain outside the assumptions.
