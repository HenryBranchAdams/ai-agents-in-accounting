# Controller orientation: how the responsibilities fit together

This is a reference route for a US family-office controller. It connects the eleven [coverage areas](COVERAGE-MAP.md) and their source-backed briefs; it is not a course, a mandated checklist, or a conclusion about a particular family. Begin by identifying the legal and beneficial owner of each asset, liability, contract, employee, bank account, investment and charitable vehicle. Then identify who can authorize actions and which reporting basis each recipient expects. The existing canonical `guide-family-office-us-accounting` and [prior research](../2026-09-24-family-office-expansion/INDEX.md) show why an office company, individual, trust, investment partnership and foundation cannot be collapsed into one book merely because one team administers them.

## Initial assessment

1. **Map people, entities and authority.** Obtain organization and ownership charts, trust/foundation governing instruments, signatory and delegation lists, adviser/service agreements, beneficiary restrictions and escalation contacts. Record effective dates and unresolved authority. Read [governance and controls](workers/governance-controls/brief.md), [trust and tax](workers/trust-tax/brief.md), and [people/philanthropy](workers/coordinator-operations/brief.md).
2. **Map money and records.** Inventory banks, custodians, managers, private-fund commitments, debt, property, significant personal assets, payroll providers and systems. For each, identify owner, currency, source statement, book/tax/reporting basis, preparer and reviewer. Read [investments and treasury](workers/investments-treasury/brief.md) and [property operations](workers/coordinator-operations/brief.md).
3. **Map outputs and deadlines.** List statutory returns, fiduciary and entity statements, management reports, tax estimates, K-1 dependencies, foundation filings, payroll duties, lender covenants, insurance renewals and family meeting packages. Note which are required by law or agreement versus a family request. Read [accounting and reporting](workers/accounting-reporting/brief.md) and [tax coordination](workers/trust-tax/brief.md).

## Recurring operating rhythm

| Cadence | Controller question and evidence |
|---|---|
| Daily or payment cycle | Are balances and near-term obligations known? Is the actual owner/payor clear? Is each bank-detail change and release separately authenticated and approved? Retain bank/custody feeds, approval logs and exception resolution. |
| Monthly close | Do bank, custodian, payroll, vendor, loan, interentity, rent-roll and private-fund schedules reconcile to each entity ledger? Are unresolved valuation and ownership items labeled? Keep trial balances, reconciliation signoffs, allocation support and an adjustment log. |
| Quarterly planning | Can forecasts cover tax payments, distributions, family spending, debt service and unfunded commitments without treating illiquid NAV as cash? Compare budget and actuals, explain large movements and refresh covenant/liquidity headroom. |
| Annual and filing cycle | Do advisers have taxpayer maps, basis records, K-1s, gift/loan/trust events, payroll and charity workpapers, property changes and source documents? Reconcile books to tax/return schedules by entity and tax year. Refresh policies, access, insurance and continuity arrangements. |

This cadence is an editorial operating proposal. It should be tailored to the family's entities, reporting promises and service providers. The sources in the briefs establish particular accounting, tax and control boundaries; they do not prescribe a universal family-office close calendar.

## Event-driven responsibilities

An acquisition, sale, capital call, loan, gift, death, incapacity, new trust, foundation grant, new employee, property casualty, payment-fraud alert or change in adviser requires a fresh owner/authority check before posting or payment. Preserve the initiating document, approvals, value and basis evidence, counterparties, terms, source statements, and both sides of related-entity entries. Route legal status and instrument interpretation to counsel; return position and elections to tax advisers; reporting measurement to the accounting specialist; valuation, insurance and security incidents to the relevant specialists. Record the advice and final approved treatment in the entity workpaper.

## Reporting principle

Maintain separate entity and fiduciary ledgers. A supplemental family dashboard can aggregate selected balances only after defining its purpose, date, included interests, valuation method, restrictions and eliminations. It must retain a bridge back to the underlying books and should show uncalled commitments, restricted trust resources, debt and valuation uncertainty distinctly. The prior [four-entity example](../aa-family-office-us-2026-09-19.md) is a carrying-value administrative illustration, not a GAAP consolidation, tax return or current-value personal statement.
