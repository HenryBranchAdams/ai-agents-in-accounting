# Prioritized family-office library expansion

This is a research-to-library handoff. It does not authorize or report canonical intake, guide editing or publication. The [coverage map](COVERAGE-MAP.md), [gap ledger](GAP-LEDGER.md) and [cumulative inventory](source-inventory.json) carry the evidence and open dependencies. Prioritize broad controller usefulness before deeper specialist exceptions.

## First drafting pass: a controller backbone

Draft a family-office controller overview with eleven linked responsibility sections and a role/entity register. Extend `guide-family-office-us-accounting` around the existing six question IDs instead of replacing its bounded accounting example. Define legal owner, beneficial interest, administrator, adviser, trustee, family member and charitable sponsor. Explain the office's service scope, authority matrix, source documents and specialist escalation. The overview should route the reader to the existing treasury, household, property, nonprofit and investments records. Keep single-family adviser-law scope separate from ownership/accounting scope.

Then add a recurring close and family-reporting policy section: entity-specific books and bases, reconciliations, interentity schedules, valuations, supplemental aggregation, budget/liquidity views and report audiences. Preserve the existing four-entity example's carrying-value label and its unresolved fund value. Every family report needs a purpose, period, perimeter, valuation method, restrictions, eliminations and bridge to underlying ledgers.

## Second drafting pass: high-frequency operating evidence

Build source-backed procedures for investment/custody and fund-capital reconciliation, a commitment-aware cash forecast, bank authority/payment changes, payroll/vendor administration, property/insurance registers and foundation/DAF distinctions. These are frequent controller tasks with direct error and fraud exposure. Reuse the six treasury workflows, `control-family-office-ownership-payments`, `src_aa_i115_irs_household_employer`, the prior `FO-O2` philanthropy research and the property role guide. Proposed control steps should be labeled editorial practices unless a source imposes a particular requirement.

## Third drafting pass: event and specialist handoffs

Add an event guide for gifts, loans, death/incapacity, new trusts, investments, property sales, foundation grants, major borrowing and cyber/payment incidents. Each event should have a minimal evidence packet and a clear handoff to counsel, tax, valuation, insurance, employment or security advisers. Separate tax basis, fiduciary income/principal, financial-statement measurement and family-dashboard values. Use actual governing instruments and contracts before stating a transaction-specific treatment.

## Intake and refresh controls

For each proposed source, resolve its exact edition and existing identity before canonical intake. The family-office package already contains 50 deduplicated references; the cumulative inventory marks reused and enriched ones. Preserve original URLs, scope, reading evidence, historical/current authority and rights uncertainty. IRS annual publications and forms, legal thresholds, securities rules, cybersecurity material, and professional manuals need dated refresh. Restricted current Codification sections and professional standards can be listed as exact access dependencies while permitted interpretations support the controller overview.

The 2022 NAIC home-insurance guide has an explicit all-rights-reserved notice on PDF page 3. Its observations support an original short summary and link, with no copied guide text or assumed reuse license.

Park the 45 [specialty-package partial questions](../2026-09-24-family-office-expansion/deferred-specialty-gaps.json) as future expansion. Also park full 50-state employment/property/charity surveys, entity-specific elections and instruments, exact family insurance adequacy, and exhaustive private-fund waterfall modeling. Reopen them when a real family scenario or source-intake decision requires it.
