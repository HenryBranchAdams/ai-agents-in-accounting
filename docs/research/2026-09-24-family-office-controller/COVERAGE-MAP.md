# Controller responsibility coverage map

Research snapshot: 2026-09-24. Each linked brief addresses the controller's recurring processes, books and reports, evidence and controls, conditional facts, and specialist handoffs. “Research route” means a reviewed path to guidance, not that these findings have been integrated into the canonical library. The six canonical family-office assessments remain partial; see [baseline verification](BASELINE.md).

| Area | Controller responsibility and existing coverage | Additional research route | Applicability and remaining gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Office structure and governance | Define service scope, legal entities, beneficial interests, delegated authority and adviser relationships. Existing `rq-family-office-perimeter` addresses selected ownership boundaries. | [Governance and controls](workers/governance-controls/brief.md) | Actual governing instruments, service agreements, family objectives and adviser status must be obtained. |
| 2. Accounting and close | Maintain entity books, reporting bases, reconciliations, allocations, interentity balances and close evidence. Existing `rq-family-office-supplemental-close` and `workflow-family-office-entity-close` are substantive for a synthetic four-entity case. | [Accounting and reporting](workers/accounting-reporting/brief.md) | Current GAAP scope, agreements, valuation and actual ledger facts remain conditional. |
| 3. Investments | Track custody, ownership, trades, fund commitments/calls/distributions, fees, income and valuation separately from performance returns. Existing `rq-family-office-capital-activity` covers selected activity. | [Investments and treasury](workers/investments-treasury/brief.md) | Actual fund documents, custody, measurement basis and NAV evidence remain conditional. |
| 4. Treasury | Position cash, forecast liquidity, fund commitments, monitor borrowing and control payments. Adjacent `treasury-cash` and six treasury workflows exist. | [Investments and treasury](workers/investments-treasury/brief.md) | Family payment authority, lender terms and bank mandates are facts. |
| 5. Trusts, estates and family transfers | Track fiduciary principal/income, beneficiary flows, gifts, loans and life events. Existing `rq-family-office-trust-beneficiary`, `FO-T1` to `FO-T3`, `FO-L1` to `FO-L2` cover selected issues. | [Trust and tax](workers/trust-tax/brief.md) | Instruments, state law, grantor status, debt terms and event facts require specialists. |
| 6. Tax coordination | Map taxpayers, tax years, basis, K-1s, estimated payments, elections and adviser deliverables. Existing `rq-family-office-tax-coordination` and prior tax briefs give selected federal routes. | [Trust and tax](workers/trust-tax/brief.md) | Tax-year forms and thresholds require refresh; state/cross-border branches and elections need facts. |
| 7. Properties and personal assets | Track title/owner, investment versus personal use, rent/deposits, costs, basis, insurance and disposition records. Adjacent real-estate guide and property workflow exist. | [Property, people and philanthropy](workers/coordinator-operations/brief.md) | State deposit law, actual use, financial-reporting basis and insurance adequacy remain open. |
| 8. People and service operations | Identify actual employer, service recipient, vendor, payer and processor; reconcile payroll and shared staff. Existing household guide and `FO-O3` cover federal employer distinctions. | [Property, people and philanthropy](workers/coordinator-operations/brief.md) | State employment/benefit rules and actual contracts require specialist review. |
| 9. Philanthropy | Separate foundation entity books, grants, investment/admin costs and returns from a donor's DAF advisory activity. Prior `FO-O2` gives role and tax boundaries. | [Property, people and philanthropy](workers/coordinator-operations/brief.md) | Foundation documents, grant facts, current GAAP and state charity rules remain conditional. |
| 10. Controls, systems and continuity | Assign approvals, access, reconciliations, provider oversight, sensitive-data protection, incident and succession routes. Existing family-office control is narrow. | [Governance and controls](workers/governance-controls/brief.md) | Small-team design and evidence depend on actual systems, roles and threats. |
| 11. Family reporting and planning | Define audiences, net-worth/entity reports, budgets, spending and liquidity, with bridges to books. Existing supplemental close is a carrying-value example. | [Accounting and reporting](workers/accounting-reporting/brief.md) | Report purpose, valuation policies and family decisions determine final presentation. |

The coverage standard is a usable route across all eleven responsibilities, not exhaustive treatment of each specialty. The [gap ledger](GAP-LEDGER.md) separates research gaps, source-access and rights limits, and entity-specific facts. The [orientation guide](CONTROLLER-ORIENTATION.md) shows how a new controller would use the material.
