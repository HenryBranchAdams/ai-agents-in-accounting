# Family-office controller reference expansion

Research handoff dated 2026-09-24. The intended reader is a capable controller starting at a US family office who needs a broad route through accounting, operations, controls and specialist handoffs. This package links the [earlier family-office research](../2026-09-24-family-office-expansion/INDEX.md) and reuses relevant evidence from the two accounting-specialty packages. It is local research; source recommendations have not been integrated into the canonical library or published.

## Read this package

- [Controller orientation](CONTROLLER-ORIENTATION.md): initial assessment, recurring rhythm, event responsibilities and report purpose.
- [Coverage map](COVERAGE-MAP.md): all eleven responsibility areas, prior coverage, additional briefs and remaining conditions.
- Area briefs: [governance and controls](workers/governance-controls/brief.md), [accounting and reporting](workers/accounting-reporting/brief.md), [investments and treasury](workers/investments-treasury/brief.md), [trust and tax](workers/trust-tax/brief.md), and [properties, people and philanthropy](workers/coordinator-operations/brief.md).
- [Cumulative source inventory](SOURCE-INVENTORY.md), [structured observations](source-inventory.json) and [inventory summary](inventory-summary.json): prior identities, new observations and reuse/correction markers.
- [Question-level gap ledger](GAP-LEDGER.md): supported routes, conditional questions and deliberately parked depth.
- [Expansion roadmap](ROADMAP.md): future intake and guide drafting order.
- [Baseline verification](BASELINE.md) and [coordinator review](REVIEW.md): evidence boundaries and validation.

The eleven areas are connected: ownership and authority determine which ledger, taxpayer, bank mandate, employment relationship, charitable vehicle and report can receive a transaction. The source packages treat recurring controller work and the specialist boundary together. The “90%” target is a breadth and prioritization instruction; no numerical adequacy score is claimed.

The reconciled handoff contains 33 question-level dispositions across all eleven areas and 90 deduplicated source references, including the prior 50. The strongest additions are a decision-rights and control map, an entity/close/reporting map, investment and liquidity operating records, a trust/tax event and dependency register, and practical property, payroll and philanthropy handoffs. Review identified exact current-GAAP access and family-specific documents as the most important limits; see [review](REVIEW.md) and [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
