# Coordinator review and evidence limits

This package is local research. The controller framework is broader than the six-question canonical guide and the earlier eleven-question family-office package. The canonical records and six `partial` assessments were compared with the prior snapshot before work; see [baseline](BASELINE.md). The earlier package's 50 source identities and the 45 deferred specialty questions are preserved.

Review criteria for this handoff:

1. Every one of the eleven responsibilities has a concrete process, a ledger or reporting route, supporting records/controls and a specialist trigger in its brief.
2. A source is mapped by original URL and edition before being counted as a new reference. Earlier research stays labeled reused; a fresh observation is not silently substituted for a prior read.
3. Official tax, securities and fiduciary material is applied to the correct taxpayer, entity and year. Historical FASB amendments and professional interpretations are not called current consolidated Codification.
4. Original operating recommendations are identified as editorial practices. Source snippets, landing pages and inaccessible text cannot support a technical claim.
5. Access, supersession, licensing, jurisdiction and family-specific agreements stay explicit. Unknown reuse rights stay unknown.

## Material boundaries to preserve

- A family dashboard and the existing synthetic four-entity close are supplemental presentations; neither proves GAAP consolidation, personal financial-statement compliance or a current-value net-worth figure.
- A trust's fiduciary income/principal split, federal taxable income, beneficiary tax character and financial reporting are different questions.
- A private fund's reported NAV, investment performance, investor tax capital and the family entity's book value are separate measures.
- A household worker's employer is determined from the actual relationship; the payroll processor and funding entity may differ.
- A private foundation is a separate organization; a DAF sponsoring charity controls contributed assets while donors may retain advisory privileges.
- Tax-year IRS publications and forms must be refreshed for the filing year. Current Codification and restricted professional material remain access or interpretation dependencies where exact sections were not inspected.
