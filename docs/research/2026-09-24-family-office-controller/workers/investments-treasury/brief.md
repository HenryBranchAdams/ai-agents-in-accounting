# Investments and treasury: controller operations

Research snapshot: September 24, 2026. This brief covers controller areas 3 and 4 and the six research questions recorded here: [custody and positions](#fo-c3-custody-positions), [fund capital](#fo-c3-fund-capital), [valuation and performance](#fo-c3-valuation-performance), [cash and liquidity](#fo-c4-cash-liquidity), [debt and collateral](#fo-c4-debt-collateral), and [payment and movement](#fo-c4-payment-movement). It adds an operating route around the existing `rq-family-office-capital-activity` and `guide-family-office-us-accounting`; it does not revise their partial assessments or the provisional value in `example-family-office-four-entity-close`.

The materials support a broad controller workflow across custody, liquid holdings, private-fund notices, direct investments, valuation, performance, banking, liquidity, debt, and payments. The family’s contracts, account mandates, books, current GAAP model, and actual valuation inputs are absent, so transaction treatment and operating effectiveness remain open. The source ledger separates prior inspected material, new current-page reads, inherited corpus references, and a discovery-only current-ASC locator.

## 3. Investments

### FO-C3-custody-positions: whose assets, held where, and under what authority?

Start with the owner and instrument register. For each account, fund interest, security, note, direct company interest, or underlying asset, capture the legal owner, beneficial interest if different, administrator, account title, custodian or issuer register, currency, governing document, transaction authority, restrictions, pledge status, and effective dates. Separate the person or entity that owns an asset from an office employee who can view it, manage records, recommend a trade, or initiate a payment. An account with an individual, trust, partnership, foundation, or service company title belongs first in that owner’s evidence trail. A family aggregation may show a beneficial interest, but it must keep the underlying owner and its separate ledger visible.

The current federal custody rule concerns registered or required-to-register investment advisers and their custody of **client** assets. It defines custody to include possession, withdrawal authority, and some GP, managing-member, or trustee roles; it then sets qualified-custodian, notice, statement, and independent-verification requirements subject to stated exceptions. It does not decide which family member owns an asset, whether the adviser-registration exclusion applies, or what belongs in GAAP consolidation. Apply the separate current family-office exclusion test from [FO-R3](../../../2026-09-24-family-office-expansion/reporting-investments/brief.md#fo-r3-sec-family-office-exclusion-and-its-separate-perimeter) only to the adviser-law question. Do not infer client assets from family assets under administration, or infer an accounting perimeter from a regulatory custody status.

Build the position book at the legal-owner and account level. For marketable securities, retain instrument and class, identifier, units or face amount, trade and settlement dates, cost lots where relevant, accrued income, price and valuation date, currency, corporate actions, restrictions, and the applicable reporting classification. Match custodian statements and trade confirms to the security subledger and general ledger; reconcile purchases, sales, income, fees, transfers, unsettled activity, and ending units. For private or uncertificated company interests, add the signed subscription or purchase agreement, issuer or transfer-agent register/cap table, class and preference rights, dilution and conversion terms, board or consent rights, and transfer restrictions. A broker line, fund portal or spreadsheet label alone does not prove legal title, control, significant influence, or accounting classification.

Useful existing routes are `workflow-family-office-entity-close`, `control-family-office-ownership-payments`, `wf-treasury-debt-investments`, and `guide-family-office-us-accounting` (`rq-family-office-perimeter` and `rq-family-office-capital-activity`). Their minimum evidentiary tests already call for account-title and beneficial-rights checks, dated statements, position and activity reconciliations, duplicate-notice review, and visible ownership exceptions. The current [SEC custody rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206%284%29-2) is relevant only after adviser status and client-asset facts are established. Its account-statement or audit exceptions are rule-specific and must not be treated as general family-office controls.

### FO-C3-fund-capital: how do commitments, calls, distributions, fees, and tax capital connect?

Maintain a fund-level subledger with the legal fund, feeder or SPV, LP/partner, commitment, signed LPA and side letter, commitment date, call notice, due date, paid date, cash account, and running unfunded balance. Record each notice’s purpose and allocation, current and cumulative calls/distributions, transaction category, remaining unfunded amount, and whether a distribution is recallable or offsets future commitments. Match the GP/administrator notice to the bank debit or credit and the investor entity’s ledger. Track the fund’s reported capital account and NAV separately from the investor’s booked amount, tax-basis capital, outside basis, and the family member’s direct ownership. A notice may create an amount due before cash clears; a cash movement may include fee, return of capital, income, realization proceeds, or recallable capital. Use the actual agreement and notice to classify it.

The [ILPA call and distribution template (R046)](../../../2026-09-23-accounting-specialties-followup/source-inventory.json) supplies useful notice fields for commitments, unfunded balances, current and cumulative flows, categories, and effects on commitments. It is a standardization aid, not a required form or accounting authority. The [ILPA model LPA (R047)](../../../2026-09-23-accounting-specialties-followup/source-inventory.json) illustrates how contractual waterfalls can return capital, preferred return, GP catch-up, carried interest, escrow, clawback, or LP giveback under a specific deal-by-deal model. Its terms and bracketed alternatives do not substitute for the actual LPA, amendments, side letters, class-specific terms, and governing law. The August 2026 KPMG investment-company handbook (R068) separately explains ASC 946 fund accounting, capital/NAV, investor classes, fees, carry, clawback, and equalization within its scope. None of those fund-level concepts automatically governs the family investor, service company, trust, or manager.

Keep book, tax, and contract schedules distinct. For partnership interests, the reused Treasury/IRS reads R040, R049, R050, R120, and R121 cover tax allocations, tax capital, outside basis, contributed-property layers, and transferee-specific basis. They do not establish financial-statement equity or fair value. Reconcile K-1 items and outside-basis changes to the tax workpaper without using K-1 capital as the ledger or investment measurement. Similarly, separate management-service fees earned by an office or manager from fund expenses, investor allocations, carried interest, and family reimbursements. R132 is a professional interpretation of fact-sensitive manager revenue/carry models; contracts, services, selected accounting policy, and current GAAP need review.

For each capital event, preserve the notice and agreement section, gross amount, date, owner, bank transaction, ledger posting, allocation, current and cumulative totals, unresolved difference, and reviewer. Match reciprocal entries only when the evidence establishes a due-to/from or another specific relationship; do not create an intercompany entry solely because funds moved between family-related accounts. Send disputed waterfalls, allocations, recallability, default consequences, or transfer rights to fund counsel, the GP/administrator, and the fund-accounting specialist before booking or paying.

### FO-C3-valuation-performance: what does a value or return measure, and for whom?

Choose the reporting entity, purpose, period, legal rights, and applicable accounting model before valuing an interest. ASC 946 scope belongs to the fund that meets its conditions; the family investor’s control, equity-method status, instrument classification, and other measurement requirements are separate analyses. The existing FO-R2 research uses R068 and a current Deloitte ASC 820 NAV source to explain the conditional NAV practical expedient: an eligible investor may use qualifying ASC 946-consistent NAV, but when the latest NAV is not aligned to the investor’s reporting date or is not calculated consistently, the investor considers whether an adjustment is needed. This is not a rule to use every late NAV or to make a formulaic adjustment. Preserve the prior partial status and keep the synthetic June NAV and September difference unresolved until current fund data and rights support a reporting-date conclusion.

For a private-fund or direct-investment value bridge, retain the fund/issuer measurement date; later calls and distributions; current underlying valuations and intervening events; accrued expenses and liabilities; investor-class allocation; and carry, waterfall, or preference support. Label each value’s source, date, method, and uncertainty. A quoted price, preliminary statement, latest NAV, tax basis, fund capital account, purchase price, and family dashboard estimate are not interchangeable. Route stale or disputed positions to the valuation lead, fund administrator/GP, and accounting specialist. Use a current Codification review for the actual reporting period, instrument, unit of account, and required disclosures; the access locator in `src_fasb_asc820_fair_value` does not itself supply those paragraphs.

Financial-statement measurement answers what a specified entity reports at an as-of date. Performance reporting answers how an explicitly defined portfolio or total fund performed over a period and how cash flows, leverage, valuation, transaction costs, underlying fund fees, and management fees enter the chosen return. The [CFA Institute GIPS standards](https://www.cfainstitute.org/-/media/documents/code/gips/2020-gips-standards-asset-owners.pdf) are voluntary performance-calculation and presentation standards for an asset owner that adopts them. The 2020 asset-owner provisions distinguish time-weighted and money-weighted returns, require policy-consistent inputs and valuations, and specify fee/cash-flow treatment. They do not turn performance returns into GAAP statements or mandate GIPS for every family. Label reports as gross or net of the stated fee layers, state benchmark and valuation policies, and preserve the bridge to source positions. Do not use realized/unrealized return, investment committee reporting, or GP performance data as a substitute for entity books.

### FO-C3 work product

The controller’s monthly investment packet should include an owner-and-account inventory; position and cash rollforwards by legal entity; trade, custodian, issuer, and fund-statement reconciliations; commitments/calls/distributions and due dates; income and fee schedules; valuation dates and open NAV/direct-investment exceptions; debt or pledge encumbrances; and a separate performance report with its perimeter and return policy. Show unreconciled units, notices, fees, or valuation inputs as open exceptions rather than forcing them into a family-wide total. Existing IDs to reuse are `guide-family-office-us-accounting`, `example-family-office-four-entity-close`, `workflow-family-office-entity-close`, `control-family-office-ownership-payments`, `treasury-cash`, `wf-treasury-debt-investments`, `guide-q-investments`, and `guide-q-funds-custody`.

## 4. Treasury

### FO-C4-cash-liquidity: what cash is available, for which entity and date?

Keep an account master for each bank, custody, brokerage, sweep, or payment account: legal title/owner, account type and permitted purpose, institution, currency, statement/feed route, authorized signers and entitlements, transaction limits, restriction or pledge, in-transit activity, and last independently reconciled date. Protect account numbers in operating workpapers and limit access to those who need it. Do not treat a bank login as title evidence, a brokerage sweep as immediately available cash, or an investment NAV as cash on hand.

Use `wf-treasury-daily-cash` to assemble opening/closing bank and book balances by entity, account, currency, availability, restriction, and value date. Reconcile each statement to its ledger account and retain cleared activity, outstanding checks/transfers, settlement items, bank errors, stale feeds, duplicate accounts, and aged differences. Confirm the complete account population, roll the opening balance through transactions to closing balance, and independently review material or unusual items. Interentity transfers must match both sides and retain the same transaction ID; an unmatched amount remains visible. Restricted cash, collateral, trust property, client assets, and balances legally owned by another entity should not be netted against an office cash need.

Use `wf-treasury-cash-forecast` to combine verified opening liquidity with dated, owner-specific inflows and outflows. Include expected receipts; operating costs and payroll; taxes; family distributions; fund calls and eligible distributions; investment settlements; debt principal, interest, and fees; capital projects; and minimum cash/availability constraints. Separate approved or contractually committed flows from estimates and scenario assumptions. Use base and stress views for amount, timing, market-value haircuts or delayed settlements, and record the forecast version, assumptions, period, currency conversion, reviewer, and variance to actual. A private-fund NAV or illiquid family asset should not be counted as available liquidity just because it appears on a performance or net-worth report.

### FO-C4-debt-collateral: which obligations constrain cash and investment assets?

Maintain a debt and credit-facility register keyed to the legal borrower and agreement. Retain executed agreements, amendments, guarantees, collateral schedules, draw requests, lender statements, notices, waivers, payoff letters, and covenant certificates. Record drawn and committed-undrawn amounts, lender, maturity, rate and reset dates, principal schedule, interest and lender fees, borrowing-base terms, collateral and lien priority, recourse/guarantors, reporting dates, covenant definitions, baskets, cure rights, default provisions, and consent conditions. Reconcile lender principal and interest to the borrower ledger and bank cash; distinguish a contingent commitment, current draw, guarantee, and pledged asset instead of treating them as one cash balance.

`wf-treasury-liquidity-covenants` already calls for extracting the operative definitions, using approved results/forecasts, recalculating the numerator and denominator, testing headroom, including amendments and waivers, and preparing a sensitivity/escalation packet. The agreement, not a generic covenant formula, determines permitted add-backs, restricted payments, eligible liquidity, collateral value, testing date, notice, grace and cure. Recalculate from traceable source amounts, retain the signoff and deliverables calendar, and stress a covenant breach or reduced borrowing base in the liquidity forecast. Route interpretation of ambiguous terms, lien priority, guarantee exposure, waiver, default, or asset-release conditions to lending counsel and the treasurer. The existing `src_fasb_asc470_debt` record is an access locator; current classification, interest, fees, covenant and disclosure paragraphs were not directly reviewed for this brief.

### FO-C4-payment-movement: how does cash move safely and reconcile afterward?

Use the already-existing `wf-treasury-bank-administration` and `wf-treasury-payment-initiation` with `control-family-office-ownership-payments`. Keep separate roles for request, preparation, approval, release, bank administration, and reconciliation to the extent the team can support it. Confirm authority for the legal owner and transaction type, then verify payee, account, amount, purpose, date, currency, supporting invoice/call/distribution or governing authorization, duplicate status, limits, and any applicable sanctions screening. A change to bank details, new beneficiary, unusual urgency, first payment, family distribution, investment settlement, or transfer across entities gets an independent callback to a previously verified contact through a trusted channel. Do not use the email or phone number in the change request as the sole verification source. Bank tokens and entitlements should be individual, least-privileged, protected by the bank’s authentication controls, and recertified when roles change.

For ACH, the [Nacha 2026 phase-two rule summary](https://www.nacha.org/rules/risk-management-topics-fraud-monitoring-phase-2) says in-scope non-consumer Originators, TPSPs, and TPSs must maintain risk-based procedures reasonably intended to identify unauthorized or false-pretense entries and review them at least annually; the exact party role and Nacha Rules control. This ACH requirement does not transfer to wires, Fedwire, checks, or other rails. The existing `src_fedocirc` source points to Federal Reserve service circulars for their covered account holders and services; the bank’s rail agreement and current governing law remain necessary for a specific transaction. The existing `src_ofaccomp` and `src_ffiecauth` sources likewise have bounded sanctions or supervised-financial-institution scope; they do not create a universal family-office screening or bank-authentication rule.

FinCEN’s [2016 BEC/EAC advisory](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/advisories/fincen-advisory-fin-2016-a003) describes executive/supplier impersonation, changed payment instructions, unusual urgency, and multi-channel verification as fraud signals and response patterns. It is guidance to financial institutions, not a universal office mandate, but supports treating email-only change requests as an exception needing independent verification. If a transaction is suspected to be fraudulent, stop the affected payment if still possible, follow the bank’s incident contact and recall process promptly, preserve instructions and logs, and involve security, counsel, and the appropriate financial institution. Recovery is not assured.

After release, retain the immutable approved payment details, initiator and approver, independent verification, rail, submission and acknowledgment/return, value date, bank statement, and ledger posting. Reconcile the transfer to its source obligation and destination account. For transfers between related entities, match both sides before classifying a loan, contribution, distribution, reimbursement, expense, or asset acquisition. A payment instruction is not proof of legal authority or accounting classification. The general control routes already link `src_1v1zwt5` (COSO), `src_0zwe7na` (NIST), `src_ffiecauth` (FFIEC), `src_nachafm` (Nacha), `src_fincenbe` (FinCEN), `src_fedocirc` (Federal Reserve), and `src_ofaccomp` (OFAC) through `wf-treasury-payment-initiation` and `treasury-cash`.

### FO-C4 work product

The treasury packet should show reconciled balances and availability by entity/account/currency; forecast horizon, committed calls and debt service, scenarios and changes; current bank signers/limits and pending changes; facility utilization, collateral, covenant headroom and deadlines; payment exceptions and fraud alerts; and aging interentity transfers. It should bridge forecast to actual cash, bank to ledger, each payment to the originating approval, and each facility calculation to the current executed agreement. A dashboard or management summary may show consolidated liquidity only after it labels restrictions, pledges, entity ownership, transfers in transit, and amounts not available for another entity.

## Applicability, gaps, and handoffs

The most transferable work is document collection, complete account/position populations, dated two-way reconciliations, explicit owner and reporting-basis labels, event-based cash forecasting, agreement-linked covenant workpapers, and independently verified payment changes. Applicability changes with the office’s adviser status, client population, legal entities, trusts, direct company rights, fund terms, statement purpose, payment rail, and legal borrower. No entity or family records, live account statements, position files, LPAs, side letters, credit agreements, bank mandates, covenant certificates, or return calculations were provided.

Route ownership, account authority, adviser status, custody exceptions, and fund rights to counsel and compliance; ASC 946/control/equity-method/instrument measurement, fees and financial statements to the accounting specialist; outside basis, K-1, allocations and tax character to the partnership tax preparer; stale NAV and direct-company valuation to the valuation specialist, GP/administrator, or auditor; return definitions and benchmark/fee calculation to the investment-performance lead; debt terms, liens, guarantees, waivers and payment disputes to lending counsel and treasury; payment system access and suspected compromise to security and bank incident teams. Do not use these handoffs to delay normal reconciliations: retain the open item and its owner/date until cleared.

Current ASC 321/323/470/820/946 text, a fund’s actual governing documents, family-specific banking and credit terms, state-law issues, cross-border rules, and professional review remain unresolved. The existing `rq-family-office-capital-activity` and all six canonical family-office question assessments stay partial. This brief contributes operational evidence only; it does not clear the 45 partial specialty questions parked in the [deferred gap ledger](../../../2026-09-24-family-office-expansion/deferred-specialty-gaps.json), close a real family’s books, or establish control effectiveness. Publisher rights remain with their owners; no source full text is stored.
