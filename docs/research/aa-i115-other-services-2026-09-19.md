# AA-I115 other services source package

This source-only package covers selected US residual branches under NAICS 811,
812, 813 and 814: repair and warranty, personal and laundry services, funeral
and pre-need arrangements, membership or religious organizations, and private
household employers. The illustrative period is calendar year 2026. US GAAP,
federal tax, and consumer-protection routes remain separate.

## Research evidence

The package adds two primary-source records. The Federal Trade Commission
detailed “Complying with the Funeral Rule” guide records its pre-need and at-need
scope, general price-list and consumer-choice provisions, and the limit that those disclosures do not decide
GAAP recognition, trust treatment, or perpetual-care accounting. The IRS
Publication 926 page records the household-employee determination, employment
tax, withholding, and Schedule H routes, as Publication 926 (2026), for use in 2026, with thresholds, state rules, and actual
worker facts left open.

Accepted FASB revenue, nonprofit presentation and contribution, IRS Form 990,
principal-agent, and NAICS source IDs are reused with their existing primary
metadata. The selected questions propagate each source URL, locator,
effective-period note, access limit, and unknown rights status.

## Selected material

The package adds a guide, evidence workflow, role and cutoff control, and two
example records. The connected synthetic cases cover repair parts and labor
with an assurance warranty estimate, laundry batch completion with
customer-goods custody, funeral pre-need deposit and service milestone,
membership dues split between benefits and contribution plus restricted
program support; the restriction release is a net-asset reclassification with no
second revenue, and household employee payroll with a contractor
counterbranch. All journals use integer cents and independently reconcile.

The six named questions and six partial assessments preserve remaining branch
dispositions. They do not assert coverage of every NAICS 81 leaf, a 50-state
survey, current consolidated Codification, actual tax filing, legal rights,
professional review, or operational evidence.

## Integration contract and validation

The helper is additive and stages writes before applying them. It preserves
existing records, source metadata, questions, assessments, and mappings;
rejects source-URL, pointer, mapping, and edition conflicts before writing; and
does not write the catalog, releases, or snapshots. The package is based on
`e3ebb24341fc8f2f8cf02b2a8d6e029bd8af32ba` and targets current corpus edition
`2026-09-19.12422`.

The focused test exercises resolved record and assessment schemas, URL identity,
connected arithmetic, positive and negative examples, additive replay,
conflict-before-write behavior, disposable applied validation, and real agent
search, context, and get retrieval. It also checks that every example record
has a nonempty synthetic scenario array.
