# Family-office expansion roadmap

This is the active research priority. The 45 partial specialty questions are [future expansion](deferred-specialty-gaps.json); both specialty packages remain unchanged. No canonical intake or publication is reported here.

## First: reporting purpose and entity register

Expand the existing family-office guide with a decision table distinguishing office-company statements, investment-entity statements, fiduciary principal/income, trust income tax, personal financial statements and supplemental family aggregation. Each output needs its own owner, included assets/liabilities, basis, period and restrictions. Preserve the existing example's carrying-value scope. The SEC exclusion answers an adviser-law question, not whether an asset belongs in an entity balance sheet.

Use the reconciled FO-R1 through R3 findings, retaining the unresolved current ASC 274 access dependency. Connect to existing `rq-family-office-perimeter`, `rq-family-office-capital-activity` and `rq-family-office-supplemental-close`; avoid inventing an adequate whole-industry assessment.

## Second: trust and investment subledgers

Develop a paired fiduciary/tax bridge for receipts, capital gains, expenses and beneficiary distributions, then a grantor/nongrantor reporting map. Add an estate-transition evidence checklist distinguishing ownership, date-of-death valuation, tax basis and income in respect of a decedent. Instrument/state rules and federal tax classification must stay separate.

Reuse prior investment-company, capital-call, partnership capital and transferee-basis research. Prior source IDs and limits are listed in [shared research](SHARED-RESEARCH.md). A stale private-fund statement, tax K-1 and current financial-statement measurement are different evidence objects.

## Third: shared costs, family transfers and philanthropy

Prepare original close schedules for reciprocal office charges, household-employer costs, family loans and gifts. Keep legal principal, cash interest, tax adjustments, donor value and recipient basis separately reconcilable. A proposed allocation driver supports cost attribution but does not prove deductibility. A compensation label does not by itself establish the employer.

Give philanthropy a distinct role branch. Identify whether the office administers a separate private foundation or the family retains only advisory privileges over a sponsoring charity's donor-advised fund. Source intake must preserve the difference between accounting recognition, charitable tax rules and restrictions on transactions with insiders.

## Source-intake criteria and remaining dependencies

An original-source URL, bounded read, edition, relevant questions, authority classification, existing-ID match and explicit rights limits support a metadata/editorial intake proposal. They do not authorize copying publisher text, establish current consolidated GAAP, or prove a treatment for a particular family. Enrich matching source records rather than duplicating them. Retained prior sources must not be presented as freshly verified.

For each proposed guide section, distinguish:

- A general question supported by inspected evidence and ready for scoped drafting.
- A source/currency gap needing an exact current section or permitted supporting interpretation.
- Entity facts such as governing instruments, elections, contracts, ownership and valuation inputs.
- Reuse limitations or unknown rights, independent of accounting applicability.

Unresolved topics stay in the question ledger with a specific next step. Source-count growth is not the goal; accurate accounting distinctions and retrievable evidence are.
