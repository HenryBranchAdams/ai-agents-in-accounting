# Family loans and gifts: evidence and separate measurement schedules

The existing family-office guide asks for transfer evidence and matches reciprocal balances, but does not substantively explain family-loan tax mechanics or a gift-to-basis bridge. This supplement deepens `rq-family-office-tax-coordination`, `rq-family-office-shared-costs` and `rq-family-office-supplemental-close`. It is research, not a recommendation to lend, forgive debt or file a return.

## FO-L1: Loan servicing versus tax adjustments

[IRS Publication 550 (2025)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550), “Below-Market Loans,” distinguishes gift/demand loans from other term loans. Forgone-interest transfers can produce tax income without cash; other below-market term loans can create an initial deemed transfer and OID. Exceptions are conditional. The loan's relationship, purpose, balance, terms and tax period therefore matter before calculating tax adjustments.

[Official 2024 §7872](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/pdf/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleF-chap80-subchapC-sec7872.pdf), (d)-(f), adds an important boundary: the $100,000 gift-loan limitation concerns the income-tax retransfer, not a blanket gift-tax exemption. Term gift loans have a separate gift-tax rule. Rate timing differs by loan type, and other debt regimes may apply. This official compilation is historical; attempts to fetch current House text failed. The [IRS AFR index](https://www.irs.gov/applicable-federal-rates) was inspected, but no applicable ruling or rate was selected. Its rendered entries omitted some months, so absence from that view does not establish nonpublication.

Original bookkeeping design: maintain signed terms, legal lender/borrower, advances, maturity, actual payments split between principal and interest, unpaid interest, modifications, forgiveness and reciprocal confirmation. Keep any tax-imputation schedule separately identifiable and reconcile it to the tax return workpaper. Neither a family label nor an AFR-matching coupon proves bona fide debt, collectibility or financial-statement carrying value.

**Original synthetic servicing example:** assume a valid $200,000 note, a full year of simple contractual interest at a hypothetical 4%, no opening accrued interest, no modifications, and a year-end $12,000 payment applied first to interest. Contract interest is $8,000; principal repayment is $4,000; ending principal is $196,000. The lender's cash receipt and borrower's payment match. This is servicing arithmetic, not an AFR, tax-imputation, fair-value or GAAP journal-entry conclusion. An impairment assessment, selected financial-reporting basis and any tax adjustment remain separate.

## FO-L2: Gift evidence and recipient basis

[2025 Form 709 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i709), “Adequate Disclosure,” support a handoff containing the asset and consideration, donor/donee relationship, relevant trust particulars, and appraisal or valuation methodology. Completing a ledger entry does not establish adequate return disclosure or whether a gift is complete.

[Publication 551, December2025](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p551), “Property Received as a Gift,” requires keeping donor adjusted basis and gift-date value distinct. A gift whose value is below donor basis may have different gain and loss bases. **Original simplified tax example:** donor basis $100,000, gift-date value $80,000, later sale $90,000, with no intervening adjustments or selling costs, produces neither tax gain nor loss under the described rule. The $80,000 valuation does not automatically replace every basis or reporting amount. Actual gift-tax adjustments and special rules remain to be evaluated.

Editorial proposal: a transfer register should link the donor's disposition, recipient's acquisition, asset identifier, quantities, dates, valuation evidence, donor-basis history, applicable adjustments and tax reporting status. Preserve source documents even when a supplemental family report eliminates the internal transfer. This avoids losing a taxable-person event inside an administrative aggregation.
