# Reuse existing partnership, fund and property research

The prior specialty packages remain preserved and their open questions remain future expansion. Family-office authors can nevertheless reuse their valid, bounded evidence. These are prior research links, not new source reads or canonical additions.

| Prior source identity | Family-office use | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| R040, 2025 Treasury §1.704-1 | Distinguish section704(b) capital from tax capital and other measures in a family investment partnership | Does not establish financial-statement equity or the actual special allocation |
| R049 and R050, 2025 Form1065 and partner K-1 instructions | Reconcile taxpayer reporting, capital and separate outside basis | Use exact tax year; the K-1 source already has canonical ID src_family_office_irs_k1_1065_2025 |
| R120, 2025 §1.704-3 | Carry contributed-property book/tax difference through depreciation, sale and liquidation | Method, property and partner facts remain necessary |
| R121, 2025 §1.743-1 | Identify transferee-specific basis layers after ownership change | Election and transaction conditions are not assumed |
| R068, KPMG 2026 Investment companies | Separate investment-company scope, fund reporting and investor measurement | Professional interpretation; fund rules do not automatically apply to an office service company or holding entity |
| R132, KPMG December2025 Revenue recognition | Separate service fees and manager carry analysis from fund allocations | Contract, role, consolidation and selected accounting matter |
| R102 and R106, lease/deposit interpretations | Handle family-owned property and tenant deposits as separate owner/manager obligations | Actual leases and local deposit laws are not supplied |

Original metadata, locators, rights and observations are in the [prior cumulative source inventory](../2026-09-23-accounting-specialties-followup/source-inventory.json). The [capital/property supplements](../2026-09-23-accounting-specialties-followup/capital-property/01-partnership-llc.md) explain their scope. Reuse source identities and link the family-office guide instead of creating duplicate publications or copying the same explanation into several role guides.

Family-office implementation should start with the already existing `guide-family-office-us-accounting`, its six question IDs, `workflow-family-office-entity-close`, `control-family-office-ownership-payments` and `example-family-office-four-entity-close`. The example's carrying-value aggregation must stay labeled supplemental; new personal-statement, trust-tax or investment-company research cannot silently change its reporting basis.
