# Existing family-office coverage and expansion map

Verified from the current canonical guide, its ten direct sources and six assessments. All six remain partial. This document maps research opportunities; it does not alter the library.

| Existing question ID | Current substantive treatment | Follow-up research |
|---|---|---|
| rq-family-office-perimeter | Maintain separate office-company, individual, trust/estate, investment-entity and underlying property or operating ledgers. Administrative authority and beneficial interests do not make all administered assets office-owned. Consolidation requires its own control and scope analysis; an explicitly defined supplemental family aggregation is a separate purpose. The historical SEC exclusion has client, ownership/control and public-holding-out conditions; a multifamily office cannot assume the single-family exclusion. | FO-R1, FO-R2, FO-R3: reporting purpose, investment-company scope, current SEC exclusion |
| rq-family-office-capital-activity | Reconcile units, transactions and cash to the account owner. For the selected quoted equity outside consolidation/equity-method scope, the historical ASC 321 amendment routes fair-value changes and dividends to earnings. Track private-fund commitment, funded calls, distributions, book carry, tax basis and dated valuation evidence separately. A commitment is not funded capital. The example retains a provisional capital-activity ledger and holds stale NAV measurement unresolved rather than calling the last notice current fair value. | FO-R2: investor measurement and NAV; reuse prior partnership/fund research |
| rq-family-office-shared-costs | Identify the beneficiary of each payroll or vendor cost, retain its allocation basis, and record reciprocal receivable/payable entries before reimbursements. Keep a separate earned management fee and its agreement. The related-party source cautions against assuming arm’s-length terms. The original fixture allocates a vendor bill using supplied time records and leaves one reimbursement outstanding. Personal spending stays in the individual ledger. Neither allocation nor reimbursement establishes tax deductibility. | FO-O1, FO-O3, FO-L1: service-company cost distinctions, household payroll and loan balances |
| rq-family-office-trust-beneficiary | For the stated Texas trust, first check the instrument and any authorized adjustment. Selected interest is income; the specified long-held bond-sale proceeds belong to principal. An accounting expense involving both income and remainder interests is split equally under the selected default provisions. The example pays an authorized income distribution and retains income separately. These fiduciary classifications do not determine GAAP presentation, DNI, the distribution deduction or beneficiary tax character. | FO-T1, FO-T2, FO-T3: fiduciary income, tax ownership and estate transition |
| rq-family-office-tax-coordination | Use separate taxpayer and tax-year identifiers. The investment partnership issues partner K-1 information; the individual and trust receive their own shares, including income not distributed. Maintain outside basis independently from K-1 capital. The non-grantor trust uses the fiduciary return and beneficiary K-1 route, while the individual uses the individual return and the assumed office C corporation retains its corporate route. Collect donor, donee, transfer-date/value and prior-gift evidence for gift-tax review, and decedent, death-date, ownership/value and prior-transfer evidence for estate review. No filing requirement, tax amount or planning action is determined. | FO-T1 to T3, FO-O1 to O3, FO-L1 to L2: trust, service, philanthropy, employment and transfer evidence |
| rq-family-office-supplemental-close | Complete separate bank, custodian and ledger reconciliations, then define the included accounts and purpose of a supplemental view. Remove duplicated interests only where the underlying entity is also included, match interentity balances, and distinguish internal fees and beneficiary transfers from external activity. Retain the entity trial balances and an adjustment log. The original four-entity example shows a balanced carrying-value aggregation, including restricted trust resources and an unresolved valuation exception; it is not an individual’s net worth or a final GAAP financial statement. Payment-authority and sensitive-data controls are editorial proposals. | FO-R1, FO-R2, FO-L1, FO-L2: basis labels, valuation and separate taxpayer events |

The philanthropic-vehicle and household-employer questions also extend the role map beyond the four entities in the existing example. Cross-border trusts, foreign reporting, insurance ownership, complex derivatives, private trust companies and state-by-state fiduciary comparisons are outside this bounded expansion. Their omission is not a conclusion that coverage is adequate.
