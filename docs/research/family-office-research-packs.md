# Scoped family-office research packs

The normal production build generates `/downloads/family-office-research-packs.json` and `/downloads/family-office-research-packs.md` from the canonical corpus for its current edition. These four small discovery routes cover selecting a reporting basis for books (FO-04), fiduciary principal/income reconciliation (FO-22), partnership investment tax basis (FO-27), and supplemental family statements (FO-33). Each provides a question, required context, canonical reading sequence, source evidence metadata, limitations and a pending reviewer handoff.

Use the JSON facets to select reporting purpose, workflow, entity, framework, jurisdiction and period. Facets are editorial routing scope, not conclusions about applicability. The period facet remains reader-selected: a displayed edition or review date is not an effective date. The selected sources' effective-period and applicability metadata are preserved when present; unknowns remain null. These packs do not perform historical filtering or silently remove sources with unknown applicability. Use the existing `as_of` retrieval with its omission reporting when a historically bounded evidence set is required, then reconcile the result with the pack's reading sequence.

Books, tax basis, fiduciary accounting and supplemental family reporting retain separate purpose values. The fiduciary route includes a federal tax bridge without converting principal/income allocations into taxable income. The partnership route keeps outside basis separate from a K-1 capital balance. The supplemental route does not confer an audit or financial-statement engagement status. Confirm the reporting basis and document bridges before combining views.

Questions and checklists come from canonical guides. Source identity, title, publisher, jurisdiction, review status, review date, rights and available locators come from current canonical source records, rather than the older embedded source-discovery annotation. The canonical topic supplies reading roles and navigation hints; their discovery locator limits remain visible. SHA-256 fingerprints for all consumed canonical records allow downstream consumers to identify changed inputs. The deterministic artifacts contain no timestamp from the build clock.

The packs introduce no new canonical records, duplicated import registry, external full text, credentials, service dependency or private family data. Publisher entry points retain source-specific access and rights limits. Discovery questions remain unanswered, reviewer identity/date/conclusion remain unknown, and generation establishes structural integrity only. Source currency, operative provisions, professional review and entity-specific conclusions remain reviewer responsibilities.

Run `node scripts/family-office-research-packs.mjs` for local files under ignored `outputs/family-office-research-packs`, or supply an output directory. `npm run build` creates the public downloads. `tests/family-office-research-packs.test.mjs` checks purpose distinctions, canonical context and reading order, current source metadata, unknown dates/rights, reference failures, deterministic output and production-build parity.
