# Follow-up expansion roadmap

This roadmap sequences future source intake and guide drafting. It does not authorize or report canonical integration. The first-pass [roadmap](../2026-09-22-accounting-specialties/ROADMAP.md) remains the foundation; this supplement will identify new evidence that changes readiness.

## Readiness rules

- **Ready for bounded drafting:** an inspected source supports the stated general question and its limitations can be carried into the prose. This does not mean entity application, professional acceptance or publication is ready.
- **Ready for metadata intake review:** source identity, original URL, scope, classification and existing-ID matches have been reconciled. Unknown external reuse rights remain unknown; source full text stays outside the corpus.
- **Needs research or access:** a material accounting assertion still lacks a permitted substantive read, has unresolved contradictory interpretations, or needs an exact current authority not available here.
- **Needs entity facts:** applying a supported principle requires actual contracts, plan terms, elections, ledgers, valuation inputs, title evidence or court orders. Do not fill those gaps with a generic source.

## Shared authoring sequence

1. Partnership capital, basis and ownership-change bridges support funds, real estate and family-office coverage. Keep financial reporting, section 704(b) capital, tax capital and outside basis separate.
2. Cost objects, normal capacity, variances and avoidable/common costs support contractor and dealership accounting. Contract reimbursement and management profitability remain distinct from GAAP inventory.
3. Small-business reporting should establish the chosen basis and engagement type before discussing close adjustments and output. Service scope does not determine tax-method eligibility.
4. Fund books and NAV precede manager/GP and private-equity/hedge specializations. Link shared sources rather than copying accounting explanations across roles.

Government contractors, dealerships, SaaS, e-commerce and oil/gas can receive bounded transaction-level expansions in parallel once their individual source packages are ready. Forensic work, bankruptcy, employee benefit plans and HOAs need separate specialty treatments; adjacent audit, employer-benefit or industry-profile records are not substitutes.

## Intake and authoring boundary

Use stable existing corpus IDs when the same source is already cataloged. Preserve prior research inventory IDs for traceability. A deeper section read generally enriches a source, while a different publication or edition may need a distinct source relationship. The cumulative inventory preserves observations instead of silently promoting authority, currency or rights.

Add explicit research questions for unresolved general issues. Put entity-specific data requirements in future guide applicability notes. Keep licensed-access and reuse dependencies separate from ordinary factual prerequisites. No source-count target or whole-specialty completeness score is proposed.

## Evidence-based readiness updates

1. **Draft foundation guides first:** partnership capital/basis bridges, normal-capacity and managerial cost distinctions, and small-business basis/service selection. The inspected tax regulations, dated KPMG inventory interpretation, IMA explanation and AICPA/IRS sources support bounded drafting. Confirm current GAAP/AR-C wording before making authoritative requirements.
2. **Expand transaction guides next:** contractor job-cost/revenue reconciliation, equipment-dealer inventory and incentives, merchant settlement-to-ledger reconciliation, and SaaS software-cost adoption. These have new section-level evidence and explicit examples. Keep issuer policies, platform mechanics, financing clauses and GAAP conclusions separately attributed.
3. **Build the shared investment/property branch:** fund NAV and investor capital, manager/GP carry, property deposits and owner settlements, then PE/VC security valuation and hedge equalization. Reuse the single KPMG revenue source and partnership foundation across funds, real estate and existing family-office material. Historical valuation/Irish administrator material supports methods only; it does not close current US authority or side-pocket entries.
4. **Draft specialized reporting branches:** debtor reporting/claim reconciliation, plan-level reporting and forensic evidence/loss methods. Keep court/regulator reporting separate from financial accounting and preserve plan-versus-employer boundaries.
5. **Hold unresolved accounting conclusions for targeted follow-up:** HOA assessment recognition needs direct current ASC and competing interpretation review; dealer departmental costing needs substantive departmental evidence; oil/gas JIB needs adopted guidance and actual agreement context; US marketplace inventory title/risk requires the applicable operative terms. Do not turn those dependencies into generic accounting rules.

All 15 supplements are ready as research handoffs. Metadata intake can be proposed for inspected sources with reconciled identities and explicit rights limits, but discovery-only and excluded-comparator observations are not ready source recommendations. Exact standard text, currency and entity facts remain gates for the specific conclusions identified in the question ledger. No general bibliography expansion is needed before these bounded authoring tasks.
