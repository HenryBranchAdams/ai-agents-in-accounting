## Limits that must survive future intake

Current Codification paragraphs were not successfully extracted for every topic; this is not proof of a paywall. Historical ASUs are not consolidated GAAP. SSARS 27 was read through its public summary, with upcoming effective-date and early-implementation conditions retained. HOA interpretations remain unresolved; issuer examples do not establish universal industry policy. IPEV and OpenStax restrictions remain intact, COPAS MFI-40 remains a draft, and vacated private-fund requirements are not revived.

Entity agreements, elections, ownership, valuation inputs and case facts cannot be settled by more generic research. External reuse rights remain unknown where not established. No publisher full text was copied into the library. See individual source observations for precise access and reuse limits.
