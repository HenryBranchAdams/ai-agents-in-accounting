# AA-I113 arts, entertainment and recreation source package

This source-only package covers selected US roles in live events, museums and
mission-based recreation, and charitable or recreation gaming. It is bounded to
an illustrative calendar-year 2026 close and keeps US GAAP research separate
from federal tax and state or local gaming questions.

## Research evidence

The new primary source is IRS, “Exempt organization gaming and unrelated
business taxable income,”
https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/exempt-organization-gaming-and-unrelated-business-taxable-income.
The packet records the Analysis and Issue indicators and audit tips sections,
including the gaming and bingo exception route, gross-receipts and prize
recordkeeping, and member or general-public distinctions. It records live
public-page access on 2026-09-19, unknown external rights, and the limit that
the page is federal tax guidance rather than a US GAAP revenue or liability
conclusion. State and local legality, licenses, entity status and actual game
facts remain open.

Accepted reusable sources retain their existing source IDs and primary
metadata. The selected questions carry source locators, source URL identity,
effective or publication-period notes, access limits and explicit currentness
boundaries. No external publisher full text is stored.

## Selected material

The package contains five records: a guide, event-to-close workflow,
role-and-settlement control record, and two examples. The examples connect
inputs to balanced journals for advance tickets, delivered services, refunds,
sponsorship or principal-agent boundaries, museum admissions and membership or
donor restrictions, gaming gross receipts, prizes and tax accrual, and a
customer-wager custody counterrole. The custody case keeps customer wagers and
remittances as a liability population and does not infer earned revenue from
cash.

There are six named questions and six partial assessments. Their remaining
gaps retain the limits around current consolidated standards, actual contracts,
rights instruments, state or local gaming law, actual filing outcomes,
professional review and operational evidence. The package does not assert
whole-subsector sufficiency.

existing source, record, question, assessment and mapping objects; refuses
publisher-URL, pointer, mapping and edition conflicts before writes; and does
not write the catalog, releases or snapshots. Package version is
`2026-09-19.113.1`, with base commit
`3f415d828e9daef343968828c0b4b7d4d4b349fe` and current corpus version
`2026-09-19.12421`.
