# Code of conduct

We want a precise, useful, and respectful project. Participate in good faith; critique claims and artifacts rather than people; disclose relevant conflicts; respect confidentiality and intellectual property; and do not use project channels for harassment, discrimination, threats, or unwanted personal attention.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct harms people or the project. Report conduct concerns through the private channel named by the project host. Do not include confidential client or employer information in a report unless the recipient is authorized to receive it.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and free from retaliation.
