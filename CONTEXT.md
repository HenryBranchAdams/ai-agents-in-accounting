# Accounting Agents glossary

## Financial-reporting capital

Capital reported under the selected financial-reporting framework. Its measurement and presentation are separate questions from federal partnership tax capital; current GAAP treatment is not established here.

## Section 704(b) capital

Capital maintained under the tax allocation rules and partnership agreement; a tax-book measure, not a synonym for GAAP equity.

## K-1 tax capital

Item L tax-basis capital reported by the partnership, excluding liability shares and section 743(b) adjustments.

## Partner outside basis

A partner’s adjusted tax basis in the partnership interest, maintained with partner-specific history and liability information.

## Common inside basis

Partnership-level adjusted tax basis in its assets, separate from transferee-specific section 743(b) layers.

## Section 704(c) layer

A property-level book-tax difference attributable to contributed property, tracked for tax allocation purposes.

## Section 743(b) adjustment

A transfer-related adjustment to partnership-property basis for the transferee alone, subject to election or statutory mandatory applicability.
